प्रश्नोत्तर

Questions people ask

Do I really pick the cards myself?

Yes. You shuffle and choose on your phone while booking; those exact cards are what Anjali reads.

Can she draw more cards in the call?

Yes. Any card she draws appears on your call screen as she turns it.

Will tarot tell me the future?

Tarot helps you see your situation clearly. Anjali won't make predictions about death, illness or exams.

Is the call recorded?

No. Your spread and her notes are kept for your next reading.
