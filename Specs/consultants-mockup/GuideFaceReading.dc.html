Mukh aakriti

Face shape and the balance of the three zones.

ल

Lalaat — forehead

Height, width and lines: thinking and early years.

ने

Netra — eyes

Shape, set and spacing: feeling and trust.

ना

Nasika — nose

Bridge and tip: drive, wealth and middle years.

ओ

Oshth and chibuk

Lips and chin: speech, steadiness and later life.

सं

Santulan — symmetry

How the two sides of your face match.

Questions people ask

Who sees my face photo?

Only Kamla ji. It is stored privately, never shown on the site, and you can ask us to delete it after your call.

Can I book palmistry and face reading together?

Yes — choose both while booking and take both photos; the session becomes 45 minutes.

Why was my photo refused?

Beauty filters, glasses, two faces or an edited picture. The camera tells you what to change.

Is the call recorded?

No. Your face map and her notes are kept for your next consultation.

गुण, दोष नहीं

About your nature, never your looks

Face reading is about character and tendencies, not beauty. Nothing about your appearance is judged or stored beyond your consultation.
