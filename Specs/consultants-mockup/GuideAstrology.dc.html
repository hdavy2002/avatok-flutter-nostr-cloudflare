Questions people ask

Can my family join the call?

Yes. Put your phone on speaker — parents often join for marriage questions.

Which language will he speak?

Punjabi, Hindi or English — choose when you book.

Is the call recorded?

No. Only your kundli and Gurdev ji's notes are kept, so your next consultation starts where this one ended.

What if Gurdev ji can't join?

You get a full refund, or pick another time — your choice.

शास्त्र सम्मत

By the book, never by fear

No scare talk and no gemstone selling on the call. Remedies are only those the shastras prescribe — mantra, daan, vrat or a puja. If a puja is advised, you can book it on Aum Fe, or at your own temple.
