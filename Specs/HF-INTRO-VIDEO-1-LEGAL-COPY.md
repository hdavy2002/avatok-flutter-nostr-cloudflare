## 1. Terms (/terms) - new section "Becoming an influencer"

1. You become an influencer in our app. You are not an influencer, and nobody can call you, until a person from our team approves you.
2. To apply you record one live intro video of 10 to 20 seconds. You cannot upload a video from your gallery. The video must show you, say what fans can call you about, and must not share phone numbers, social media handles, payment details or any other contact details.
3. Our team may approve or reject your application. If we reject it, we tell you why on WhatsApp, and you can fix it and apply again. We do not have to give a second chance if the same problem repeats.
4. You promise that you are the person in the video, that you are 18 or older, and that everything you tell us is true. Aadhaar verification is needed when you have no verified social media account, or when you choose Listener, Prayer, Virtual friend or Women-to-Women.
5. You give us permission to show your approved intro video, your name, your categories, your rate and your verified social media icons in our Reels, on our website and in our app, and to let fans share a link that plays the video. You can ask us to remove your intro video; if you do, your Reel is hidden until a new video is approved.
6. Call options. You choose Bookings only or Bookings + live, and you can change it on your dashboard. With Bookings only we never ring you except at a time someone has booked. With Bookings + live we may ring you when you are Online and free. After each queued call you get a 2 minute break.
7. Women-to-Women channels are only for women. The influencer and the caller must both be Aadhaar-verified women. We may refuse or end access for anyone who does not meet this.
8. Social media verification is optional. A green tick on your profile means our team approved you. Social media icons show only accounts you logged in to and proved are yours.
9. Existing influencers must record an intro video and be approved again. Until then, their Reel is hidden.

## 2. Privacy (/privacy) - additions

- **What we collect:** your intro video and its still picture (poster), the language you chose, your categories, your call option, and the result of our team's review (approved or rejected, and the reasons). When you use Social media verification we receive, from YouTube, Instagram or Facebook, only what is needed to show that the account is yours (account name, link and a verified flag). We do not post anything for you.
- **Aadhaar:** we use it only to check who you are and, for Women-to-Women, that the gender shown is female. We keep only what the law and our verification partner require; we do not keep your full Aadhaar number.
- **Who sees your intro video:** our review team sees it before approval. After approval it is public in Reels and on your profile. A rejected or replaced video is not public.
- **How long we keep it:** an approved video stays until you remove it or close your account. Rejected and replaced videos are deleted after [OWNER TO CHOOSE: e.g. 30 days].
- **Where it is stored:** on our cloud storage (Cloudflare R2) in a secure way, and shown through our own servers.
- **WhatsApp:** we message you on WhatsApp when your application is approved or rejected, and about your intro video if it is missing.
- **Likes, comments, shares:** when a fan likes, comments on or shares a Reel, we store that activity with their account.

## 3. Community guidelines (/community-guidelines) - additions

Intro video rules (what makes us reject a video):
- Your face must be clearly visible and the video must be bright enough to see.
- Your voice must be clear.
- Do not share phone numbers, social media handles, UPI IDs or any other contact details in the video.
- Say what fans can call you about, in your own words. Do not make promises like "I will fix your life" or give medical, legal or money advice.
- Nothing sexual, abusive, hateful, violent or illegal. No children in the video unless it is clearly safe and the guardian is present.
- The video must be live and recorded now. We reject videos that are re-recorded from another screen.
- Pick categories that match what you really do. A wrong category can lead to rejection.
- Women-to-Women: only women may start or call these channels.

Reel rules for fans:
- Likes and comments follow the same respect rules as calls: no abuse, no sexual talk, no sharing contact details.
- We may remove comments and take action on accounts that break the rules.
