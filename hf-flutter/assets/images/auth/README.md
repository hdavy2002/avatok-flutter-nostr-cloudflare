# Authentication photo assets

These original, project-bound photos were generated with the built-in image
generator on 2026-10-12. The two private 2×2 identity atlases used while
prompting were references only and are not shipped.

Shared prompt: photorealistic editorial lifestyle photography; a consistent
fictional young Indian woman and man; bright contemporary cafe/home interior;
50 mm lens; realistic skin and fabric texture; soft daylight; warm, welcoming
and trustworthy; a single continuous scene; no text, letters, digits, logos,
watermarks, UI, icons, stickers, grids, frames or malformed hands.

Final scene prompts:

- `welcome.png`: woman in a lavender knit top, chin resting on one hand,
  looking slightly upward with a hopeful smile; vertical medium portrait.
- `login.png`: woman in coral-pink and man in blue denim, shoulder-to-shoulder,
  each looking at their own phone; wide waist-up portrait.
- `second-whatsapp.png`: woman in mustard-yellow and man in blue denim facing
  camera with two phones and one plain blank SIM card; wide waist-up portrait.
- `whatsapp-otp.png`: woman in lavender looking down at a teal phone held in
  both hands; vertical medium portrait.
- `email.png`: man in blue denim looking down at a black phone held in both
  hands; vertical medium portrait.
- `email-otp.png`: woman in coral-pink looking down at a black phone held in
  both hands; vertical medium portrait.
- `name.png`: woman in mustard-yellow, chin resting on one hand, facing camera;
  vertical medium portrait with overlay space.
- `username.png`: woman in olive-green taking a cheerful selfie; vertical
  medium portrait with overlay space.
- `kyc.png`: woman in coral-orange and man in light blue leaning together over
  one shared phone; wide waist-up portrait.
- `policies.png`: woman in lavender and man in sage-green leaning together over
  one shared phone; wide waist-up portrait with overlay space.

All UI copy, stickers, icons, cards, progress bars and controls are rendered by
Flutter rather than baked into the raster assets.
