{{brand.name}} is for friendly, respectful talk between fans and influencers. Most topics are fine. A few things are never allowed. Read this page once so you know the rules.

## What you can talk about

- Their videos, reels and posts. Which one did you love?
- How they started their channel.
- Travel, food, fitness, gaming, beauty, fashion, music or movies.
- Tips on studies, career or hobbies, from their own experience.
- A shout-out, a compliment or a question you always wanted to ask.
- Cricket, books, or anything light and friendly.

**Example:** Kabir wants to start a gaming channel. He talks to a gaming YouTuber and asks how they got their first 1,000 subscribers. That is a good call.

## What is not allowed

- Spam, abuse, threats and gaali.
- Asking for or sharing phone numbers, WhatsApp, Instagram or UPI IDs.
- Planning to meet in person.
- Selling anything. This includes products, remedies, tips and loans.
- Asking for money or payment outside the platform.
- Anyone under 18.
- Asking for OTP, UPI PIN, card or bank details, or Aadhaar number.

If someone does these, press # to end the call and block them, and report it. Our team reviews reports and can give strikes or bans. See [Community guidelines](/community-guidelines).

## Sexual talk

Sexual or suggestive talk is **not allowed**.

There is one exception. It is a real health or medical talk with an influencer who has agreed to that topic. The topic is shown on the influencer's profile. Women's health talk with an influencer who lists it is one example (see [women's safety](/women-only)). Explicit or erotic talk is never allowed, even then.

## Not professional advice

An influencer is a creator you like. An influencer is not a doctor, lawyer, counsellor, CA or money advisor. Do not ask for treatment, legal steps or investment tips. For these, please meet a qualified person.

## If you need urgent help

{{brand.name}} cannot handle emergencies.

- **In danger:** call 112.
- **Mental health support:** call Tele-MANAS on 14416. It is free and open 24 hours.

If you talk about hurting yourself, it is never a strike. We only follow up to check on your welfare. See [emergency help](/emergency).
