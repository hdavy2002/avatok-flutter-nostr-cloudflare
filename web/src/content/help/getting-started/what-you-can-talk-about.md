{{brand.name}} is for friendly, respectful talk. Most topics of daily life are fine. A few things are never allowed. Read this page once so you know the rules.

## What you can talk about

- Your day, your family, your work, your studies.
- Exam stress, interview worry, shaadi tension.
- Feeling alone, or feeling low after a hard day.
- Movies, cricket, music, food, travel, books.
- Learning a language or sharing a hobby.
- Late-night talk when you cannot sleep.

**Example:** Kabir is nervous about a job interview. He talks it through with a host. He practises how to say "tell me about yourself". That is a good call.

## What is not allowed

- Abuse, threats and gaali.
- Asking for or sharing phone numbers, WhatsApp, Instagram or UPI IDs.
- Planning to meet in person.
- Selling anything. This includes products, remedies, tips and loans.
- Asking for money or payment outside the platform.
- Recording the other person.
- Anyone under 18.
- Asking for OTP, UPI PIN, card or bank details, or Aadhaar number.

If someone does these, AI can end the call by itself and flag the account. See [Community guidelines](/community-guidelines).

## Sexual talk

Sexual or suggestive talk is **not allowed**.

There is one exception. It is a real health or medical talk with a host who has agreed to that topic. The topic is shown on the host's profile. Women's health talk in the [women-only space](/women-only) is one example. Explicit or erotic talk is never allowed, even then.

## Not professional advice

A host is a friend who listens. A host is not a doctor, lawyer, counsellor, CA or money advisor. Do not ask for treatment, legal steps or investment tips. For these, please meet a qualified person.

## If you need urgent help

{{brand.name}} cannot handle emergencies.

- **In danger:** call 112.
- **Mental health support:** call Tele-MANAS on 14416. It is free and open 24 hours.

If you talk about hurting yourself, it is never a strike. We only follow up to check on your welfare. See [emergency help](/emergency).
