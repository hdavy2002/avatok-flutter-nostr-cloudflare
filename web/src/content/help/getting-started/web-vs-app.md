## Two surfaces, one account

avaTOK works across two places: the website at **avatok.ai** and the **avaTOK app**. They share the same account and the same listings — a listing looks identical whether someone finds it on the web or in the app — but they aren't built to do the same things.

## What you can do on the website

avatok.ai is the consume-side of avaTOK: you can **browse** the [marketplace](/marketplace), **book** a 1:1 session or join a live event, **pay** for it, **watch** a live show right in your browser, and take a paid **consultation** — all without installing anything. Signing up also happens here, and checkout for bookings and tickets happens here.

## What needs the app

Two things live only in the avaTOK app:

- **Messaging.** avatok.ai has no chat or messaging feature. If you've booked with a creator or want to reach out, the website hands you their **avaTOK number** plus a link to download the app — messaging itself happens there, not on the web.
- **Onboarding and your avaTOK number.** Creating an account on the website gets your account open, but the rest of onboarding — including choosing the avaTOK number that other people see instead of your real phone number — happens the first time you open the app. See [Create your account](/help/getting-started/create-your-account) for the sign-up steps.

## Why it's split this way

The website is built to be the fast, public front door — something anyone can open in a browser and use in a minute, no install required, to find a creator, watch a show or pay for a session. The app is where the fuller, ongoing relationship lives: talking to a creator, managing your avaTOK number, and everything that needs to stay with you rather than a browser tab.

## A typical path

1. Find a creator or a show on avatok.ai and book or join it.
2. Pay for it on the website — checkout happens there.
3. Watch or attend right in your browser if you like.
4. To message the creator, or if you're a creator getting paid, open the **avaTOK app** — that's where your avaTOK number lives and where the conversation continues.
