Creating an account on {{brand.name}} is free and quick. You do not need a password. You only need to be 18 or older.

## Sign up in simple steps

1. Open the website and tap **Create an account**.
2. Enter your name, your email and your WhatsApp number.
3. We send one code to your email and one code on WhatsApp. These are called OTPs.
4. Type both codes. Your account is ready.

Next time, you log in with just your WhatsApp number. We send a code on WhatsApp and you type it. There is no password to remember or lose.

You can also tap **Continue with Google** on the log-in or sign-up page. Google only proves who you are. Before you like, comment or call, we still ask you to verify your WhatsApp number once with a code.

If your email already has an account, log in with your WhatsApp number instead.

**Never share your OTP with anyone.** {{brand.name}} will never ask for your OTP, UPI PIN, password or bank details. Not on a call, not on WhatsApp, not on email.

## Only 18 and above

You must be 18 or older to join. Read our [age policy](/age-policy).

## Caller or influencer?

- A **caller** is a fan who talks to influencers and pays per minute. You can start right after sign up.
- An **influencer** takes calls from fans and earns money. Upgrade for free from your dashboard. For now, influencers must be based in India.
- You can be **both** on one account.

If you want to join as an influencer, see [Join & earn](/hosts/join).

## What stays private

An influencer's real name and phone number are never shown on their profile. They are never shared with you. The same is true for you. Your name, number and details are not shown to the influencer.

Your phone number is never shared with the other person on a call. See [How does a call work?](/help/getting-started/how-a-call-works).

## Your profile

Add your name, city and languages any time. Every account also gets a handle. See [What is on my profile, and what is a handle?](/help/account-and-help/your-profile-and-handle).

## Leaving later

You can delete your account any time. Withdraw your wallet money with the Withdraw button first. Steps are at [data deletion](/data-deletion).
