You choose who you talk to. You can look by mood, language and price. Then you check the profile and call.

## Start with a mood

Moods tell hosts what kind of talk you want. Some examples:

- Exam ki tension
- Interview anxiety
- Shaadi ka tension
- Can't sleep at night
- Naye dost (new friends)
- Bas baat (just talk)
- Talk about a subject
- A rough day
- Loneliness

## Use the filters

You can narrow the list with these filters:

- **Mood:** what you want to talk about.
- **Language:** each host lists their languages. Hindi, English, Marathi, Kannada and more.
- **Price:** each host sets their own rate per minute. Prices start from ₹5 a minute.
- **Online now:** show only hosts who can take a call right now.

## How to read a profile

Each profile shows:

- **Display name.** Not the host's real name.
- **Video-KYC badge.** The host has done a face check on video.
- **Moods and languages.**
- **Price per minute.** You see it before you call.
- **Rating.** Marks from other callers.
- **People talked to.** How many people the host has already talked to.
- **Topics the host has agreed to.** These are the subjects the host is happy to talk about.

## Favourites and Notify me

Tap the heart to save a host as a favourite. If a favourite host is offline, tap **Notify me**. We tell you when they come online. See [Favourites and calling again](/help/calling/favourites-and-calling-again).

## Example: choosing

Meena from Nashik feels low after a hard day. She picks the mood "a rough day". She filters for Marathi and "online now". She sees three hosts. One charges ₹5 a minute and has a good rating. Another has talked to many people and charges ₹10. Meena picks the first one. She will try a short call first. If she likes it, she hearts the host as a favourite.
