You choose who you talk to. Look for the creator you follow, or find a new one. Then check the profile and call.

## Find your creator

Search for the influencer by name, or browse the list. You can find YouTubers, Instagram creators, Facebook creators and more. Think of the topics you love: fitness, travel, cooking, gaming, beauty, career tips.

## Use the filters

You can narrow the list with these filters:

- **Language:** each influencer lists their languages. Hindi, English, Marathi, Kannada and more.
- **Online now:** show only influencers who can take a call right now.

## How to read a profile

Each profile shows:

- **Display name.** Not the influencer's real name.
- **Languages.**
- **Price per minute.** The influencer sets this. You see it before you call.
- **Rating.** Marks from other callers.
- **People talked to.** How many people the influencer has already talked to.
- **Topics the influencer has agreed to.** These are the subjects the influencer is happy to talk about.

## Favourites and Notify me

Tap the heart to save an influencer as a favourite. If a favourite influencer is offline, tap **Notify me**. We tell you when they come online. See [Favourites and calling again](/help/calling/favourites-and-calling-again).

## Example: choosing

Meena from Nashik loves a cooking creator who speaks Marathi. She filters for Marathi and "online now". She finds her favourite creator. She checks the price per minute and sees the topics. She wants to ask about one recipe from a video. She will try a short call first. If she likes it, she hearts the influencer as a favourite.
