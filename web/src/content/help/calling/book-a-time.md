Booking is the best way to talk to a busy influencer. You pick a time, and we ring you both at that time.

## How to book

1. Open the influencer's page and tap **Book a time**.
2. Pick a **day**. Days with open times are highlighted.
3. Pick a **time**.
4. Pick a **length**: 15 minutes, 30 minutes, 1 hour, 2 hours, 3 hours, 4 hours or 5 hours.
5. Check the rate and your wallet, then confirm. If your wallet is low, top up first.

Your phone number stays private, as on every call.

## What is held, and what you pay

When you book, we hold the cost of the **first 15 minutes** (or the whole call if you book 15 minutes). During the call the rest is charged **5 minutes at a time**, and you pay only for the minutes you speak. Unused money comes back to your wallet. See [How call money works](/help/wallet-and-payments/how-call-money-works).

## Waiting for approval

Some influencers accept each request first. Then your booking shows **Waiting for approval**. The time is kept for you, and your money stays held.

- If she **accepts**, your booking is confirmed.
- If she **declines**, or does not answer in time, you get a **full refund to your wallet**.
- You can **withdraw** a pending request any time, with a full refund.

A request that is still waiting is not a call yet, so nobody rings.

## Cancelling

- Cancel **free until 1 hour before** the call. The hold comes back to your wallet.
- Cancel later than that, and the hold is kept.
- If the influencer cancels, or does not pick up at the booked time, you always get the **full hold back**.

We WhatsApp you a reminder about 1 hour and about 10 minutes before the call.

## Booked calls and her mode

A confirmed booking rings at its time even if the influencer has switched to Offline. See [Online, Bookings only or Offline](/help/hosts/online-bookings-only-offline). If she is busy right now and you do not want to wait, you can also [join her queue](/help/calling/join-the-queue).
