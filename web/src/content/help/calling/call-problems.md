Most calls go well. If yours did not, check the points below. Most problems are easy to fix.

## My phone did not ring

Check these things one by one:

1. **Is your number right?** Check it in your account. A wrong digit means no ring.
2. **Is your network working?** Look at your signal bars. Try a different spot.
3. **Is DND on?** Do Not Disturb can stop calls.
4. **Do you use a spam-blocker app?** Some apps block unknown numbers. Our call comes from a platform number, so it may look unknown. Allow unknown numbers for a while, or add the number to your contacts.
5. **Is your phone on silent or flight mode?**

If the host said yes but your phone did not ring, you pay ₹0. Nothing is charged until both of you are connected.

## My call dropped

Calls can drop if the network is weak. **Charging stops at the moment of disconnect.** You pay only for the minutes you were connected.

Then call again. You need at least 2 minutes of balance to start. See [How much does a call cost?](/help/calling/price-and-minutes).

## The voice is bad

- Move to a place with better signal.
- Go to a quieter place.
- Check that your phone's speaker or mic is not blocked.
- Try hanging up and calling again.

If it stays bad, try another host and tell us the call ID.

## The host declined

If the host pressed 2, or did not answer in 10 seconds, you pay **₹0**. Hosts can decline any call. It is not about you. They may be busy. Try another host, or tap **Notify me** on a favourite. See [Favourites and calling again](/help/calling/favourites-and-calling-again).

## Charged by mistake?

If you were charged for a call that never connected, or charged twice, send us the details. The wrong amount is put back in your wallet. See [refund policy](/refunds).

## Contact support

Write to support (@) {{brand.domain}}. Please include:

- the call ID,
- the date and time,
- what went wrong, and the amount if it is about money.

**Never send your OTP, UPI PIN, Aadhaar number or card details.** We will never ask for them.

You can also check the [FAQs](/faq). If a person broke a rule, use [report form](/report).
