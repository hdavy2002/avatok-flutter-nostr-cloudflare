You are always in control of a call. You can end it at any second. If something feels wrong, you can use the # key.

## Hang up any time

Just hang up your phone, like any normal call. The host can do the same.

- You do not need a reason.
- You do not need to say sorry.
- Charging stops when the call ends.

Payment never buys permission to cross a boundary. If you feel uneasy, you can hang up.

## The # key

Press the **# key (hash key)** at any time during the call. Three things happen:

1. **The call ends.**
2. **The other person is blocked.** They cannot call you again, and you cannot call them.
3. **The incident is reported.** Our trust and safety team looks at it.

You do not need to speak to anyone after. One key press does all three.

## When to use hang up and when to use #

**Hang up** when the call is just not right for you. Maybe you do not enjoy the talk. Maybe you are busy. Maybe the voice is not clear.

**Press #** when someone breaks a rule or makes you feel unsafe. For example:

- abuse, threats or gaali,
- sexual pressure,
- asking for your OTP, UPI PIN or bank details,
- asking for your number, WhatsApp or Instagram,
- asking you to send money or to meet.

## Example

Priya gets a call from a host who begins to talk well. After 5 minutes he says he is from her bank. He asks her to "verify" her account with an OTP. Priya knows that is a scam. She presses #. The call ends. He is blocked and reported. The AI had also seen this, and the account was flagged.

## Good to know

- AI also follows every live call. It can end a call by itself if it finds abuse or a scam.
- Three confirmed strikes lead to a permanent ban. Serious cases can be banned at once.
- If the AI made a mistake, tell us at [grievance page](/grievance). A person will look at it.
- You can also report later at [report form](/report).

In real danger, call 112. See [emergency help](/emergency) and [safety centre](/safety).
