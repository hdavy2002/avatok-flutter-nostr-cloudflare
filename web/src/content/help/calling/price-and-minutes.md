You pay per minute, from your prepaid wallet. Each host sets their own rate. The rate is shown on their profile before you call. Prices start from ₹5 a minute.

## Billed per started minute

You are charged for each **started** minute of connected time. If you start a new minute, you pay for that whole minute.

- 61 seconds at ₹5 a minute is 2 minutes. That is **₹10**.
- 3 minutes 20 seconds at ₹10 a minute is 4 minutes. That is **₹40**.
- 45 seconds at ₹5 a minute is 1 minute. That is **₹5**.

Tip: if you are close to the end of a minute, finish your sentence and say bye. A new minute starts the moment the old one ends.

## Minimum balance to start

You need at least **2 minutes** of balance at that host's rate.

- ₹5 a minute host: you need ₹10.
- ₹20 a minute host: you need ₹40.

If your balance is lower, top up first. See [Make your first call](/help/calling/make-your-first-call).

## When charging starts

**Charging starts only when both of you are connected.**

You pay ₹0 when:

- the phone is only ringing,
- the host declines,
- the host does not answer in 10 seconds, or
- the call never connects.

## The 60-minute limit

A call lasts as many whole minutes as your balance buys. The most is 60 minutes per call.

About 60 seconds before the limit, you hear a warning. Then the call ends by itself. Your balance never goes below zero. To keep talking, top up and call again.

## Check your charges

Every top-up and every call charge shows in your wallet history. It shows the date, the length and the amount.

## Refunds and mistakes

Connected calls are not refunded. You choose the host, you see the price, and you can hang up at any second. If a call was not what you hoped, rate the host and try someone else.

If we charged you for a call that never connected, or charged you twice, tell us. That is a billing error. The wrong amount is put back in your wallet. Read [refund policy](/refunds) and [wallet terms](/wallet-terms).

Your unused wallet balance is always yours. You can send it back to your own UPI ID from the dashboard at any time.
