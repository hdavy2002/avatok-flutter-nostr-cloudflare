You pay from your prepaid wallet. Each influencer sets their own price per minute. You see the price before you call.

## The price

The price is the **rate per minute** that the influencer has set. Different influencers have different rates. Check the profile each time, because an influencer can change their rate.

## Billed by the minute

You pay by the minute while you are connected, at the influencer's per-minute rate.

**Example:** an influencer's rate is ₹10 a minute.

- A 5-minute call costs ₹50.
- A 12-minute call costs ₹120.

## Minimum balance to start

You need **at least ₹20**, or the cost of **5 minutes** at the influencer's rate if that is more, to start a call. The same is needed to join a [queue](/help/calling/join-the-queue).

If your balance is lower, add money first. See [Make your first call](/help/calling/make-your-first-call).

## When charging starts

**A call is made once we have rung both of you and joined the call.** You pay per minute spoken.

You pay nothing when:

- the phone is only ringing,
- the influencer declines or cancels,
- the influencer does not answer, or
- the call fails before we join you.

If a joined call breaks within the first minute, we charge ₹1 and return the rest to your wallet.

## How long can a call be?

A call can last up to **5 hours**. It goes on until either person hangs up or your wallet money runs out.

If your wallet is about to run out, only you hear a short message that tells you how many minutes are left. Top up in the app or on the website while you keep talking, and the call continues. Your balance never goes below zero. See [How call money works](/help/wallet-and-payments/how-call-money-works).

## Check your charges

Every addition to your wallet and every call charge shows in your wallet history. It shows the date, the length and the amount in rupees.

## Refunds and mistakes

We do not refund calls that were made, except under the first-minute rule above. You choose the influencer, you see the price, and you can hang up at any second. If a call was not what you hoped, rate the influencer and try someone else.

If we charged you twice, or for a call that was never made, tell us. That is a billing error. The wrong amount is put back in your wallet and linked to that call. All refunds go to your wallet, never to your card or UPI. Read [refund policy](/refunds) and [wallet terms](/wallet-terms).

You can take wallet money out any time with the Withdraw button. See [withdraw your wallet money](/help/wallet-and-payments/withdraw-your-balance).
