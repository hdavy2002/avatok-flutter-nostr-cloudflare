Many fans find one influencer they love and call again and again. Favourites make it easy.

## Call your favourite again

A second call feels easy. You can say, "Hi, I watched your new video," and carry on from there.

Some fans call once a week. Some call when a new video comes out. Some call when they need one more tip.

## Save a favourite

1. After a call you liked, open the influencer's profile.
2. Tap the **heart**.
3. The influencer is now in your favourites on the website.

Your wallet, favourites and call history are all on the website. You do not need an app.

## Notify me

Favourite influencers are not always online. An influencer can go online or offline any time.

If your favourite is offline, or only takes bookings:

1. Open their profile.
2. Tap **Notify me**.
3. We send you one WhatsApp when they come online.

Then you can call at once. If they are already on a call, you can also [join their queue](/help/calling/join-the-queue).

Influencers can see how many people have hearted them and asked to be notified.

## Your numbers stay private, every time

You can talk to the same influencer 50 times. Your phone number is still hidden. So is theirs. Every call goes through a platform number. See [How does a call work?](/help/getting-started/how-a-call-works).

Please do not ask for the influencer's number, WhatsApp or Instagram. Asking is not allowed, and it can end the call.

## The rules are the same on the 50th call

A close bond does not change the rules.

- Be kind and respectful.
- No abuse, no gaali, no pressure.
- No sexual talk.
- No asking for money, and no selling anything.
- No planning to meet.

Influencers may also decline any call. An influencer who says no on one day is not saying no to you for ever. Try again later, or choose another influencer.

If an influencer asks you for money outside the platform, report it at [report form](/report). Influencers who do this are removed.

## Check the price each time

Each influencer sets their own price per minute, and it can change. You see it on their profile before you call. You need at least ₹20 in your wallet, or the cost of 5 minutes at her rate if that is more, to start a call. See [How much does a call cost?](/help/calling/price-and-minutes).
