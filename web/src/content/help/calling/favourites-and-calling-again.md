Many callers find one host they like and talk to them again and again. We call this a regular "dost". Favourites make it easy.

## Build a regular dost

A good talk feels easy. You do not have to explain your story again. With a regular dost, you can say, "Hi, today was tough," and carry on.

Some callers talk once a week. Some call before every exam. Some call after a long day.

## Save a favourite

1. After a call you liked, open the host's profile.
2. Tap the **heart**.
3. The host is now in your favourites on the website.

Your wallet, favourites and call history are all on the website. You do not need an app.

## Notify me

Favourite hosts are not always online. A host can go online or offline any time.

If your favourite is offline:

1. Open their profile.
2. Tap **Notify me**.
3. We tell you when they come online.

Then you can call at once.

## Your numbers stay private, every time

You can talk to the same host 50 times. Your phone number is still hidden. So is theirs. Every call goes through a platform number. See [How does a call work?](/help/getting-started/how-a-call-works).

Please do not ask for the host's number, WhatsApp or Instagram. Asking is not allowed, and it can end the call.

## The rules are the same on the 50th call

A close bond does not change the rules.

- Be kind and respectful.
- No abuse, no gaali, no pressure.
- No sexual talk.
- No asking for money, and no selling anything.
- No planning to meet.

Hosts may also decline any call. A host who says no on one day is not saying no to you for ever. Try again later, or choose another host.

If a host asks you for money outside the platform, report it at [report form](/report). Hosts who do this are removed.

## Check the price each time

Each host sets their own rate. Check the price on the profile before you call. You need at least 2 minutes of balance. See [How much does a call cost?](/help/calling/price-and-minutes).
