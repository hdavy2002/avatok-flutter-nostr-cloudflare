Your first call takes only a few minutes to set up. Here is the whole way, step by step.

## Before you call

1. [Create your account](/help/getting-started/create-your-account) if you have not yet.
2. **Top up your wallet.** Open your wallet and add money. Your wallet holds your talk-time money in rupees.
3. Make sure you have enough balance. You need enough for at least 2 minutes at the influencer's price per minute.

## Pick an influencer and check the price

1. Search for your favourite influencer, or choose a language and "online now". See [Find someone to talk to](/help/getting-started/find-someone-to-talk-to).
2. Open a profile. Read the topics the influencer agreed to.
3. **Check the price.** The influencer sets the price per minute. You see it on the profile before you call.

## Tap call

Tap the call button. Then wait. Here is what happens and what you will hear:

1. The influencer's phone rings first. The influencer says yes or no.
2. If the influencer says yes, **your phone rings**. Pick up.
3. You hear a short safety notice. Both people hear it.
4. Then you are joined, and you can talk.

Your number stays private. See [How does a call work?](/help/getting-started/how-a-call-works).

If the influencer says no or does not answer in 10 seconds, you pay ₹0. Pick another influencer.

## First-call tips

- **Keep your phone with you and not on silent.** You must pick up when it rings.
- **Be in a quiet place.** The voice will be clearer.
- **Say hello and say what you need.** For example: "I loved your Goa travel video. Can I ask you about your budget?"
- **Start with a short call.** You can always call again.
- **Do not share private details.** Skip your full name, address, workplace, social media and bank details.
- **Remember the exit.** You can hang up at any second. Press # to end the call and block the person if something feels wrong.

## After the call

Rate the influencer. Your wallet history shows the date, time and amount. If you liked the influencer, tap the heart to save them. Learn more in [Favourites and calling again](/help/calling/favourites-and-calling-again).
