## A suggestion, nothing more

Sometimes a Guide may suggest a puja or havan from our list that fits what you talked about.

## You never have to book

It is only a suggestion. You can ignore it, think about it, or ask the Guide to explain. There is no pressure and the talk goes on the same either way.

See [How to choose and book a havan or puja](/help/booking-and-paying/how-to-book) and [Puja or havan?](/help/getting-started/puja-or-havan).
