## The seven chakras

| Chakra | Where | Bija mantra | Colour on our designs (modern scheme) | Often associated with |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Muladhara | Base of spine | Lam | Red | Grounding, stability |
| Svadhisthana | Below the navel | Vam | Orange | Creativity, flow |
| Manipura | Navel / solar plexus | Ram | Yellow | Confidence, will |
| Anahata | Heart | Yam | Green | Love, compassion |
| Vishuddha | Throat | Ham | Blue | Speech, truth |
| Ajna | Between the brows | Om | Indigo | Insight, focus |
| Sahasrara | Crown | none | Violet / white | Stillness, connection |

**About the colours.** This rainbow scheme is the one most people know today, and the one T-shirt printing works with best. It is a **modern** convention. Classical texts such as the *Satchakra Nirupana* describe the chakra lotuses in different colours. We say this openly, and Pandit ji will too if you ask.

## Mandalas and yantras

- **Mandala**: a circular sacred pattern, often with a deity, a lotus or the Navagraha (nine planets) at the centre.
- **Yantra**: a precise geometric diagram for a deity or purpose, such as the **Sri Yantra** (Lakshmi, Tripura Sundari), the **Navagraha Yantra** or the **Hanuman Yantra**.

Pandit ji mentions a mandala or yantra design when the deity or planet at its centre matches what came up in your conversation.

## How these designs are matched

In the same order as every design: deity or symbol first, then chakra, mandala or yantra, then print colour, and fabric colour last. See [Why was I suggested this design?](/help/talk-to-a-guide/why-this-design).
