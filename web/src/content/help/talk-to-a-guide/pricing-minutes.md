## Paid from your wallet

Voice talks are paid from your wallet. In the wallet, **1 token = ₹1**. See [Your wallet and tokens](/help/talk-to-a-guide/wallet-and-tokens).

## What each Guide costs

- **Meera (Astrology, by voice):** **₹6 per started minute**, which is 6 tokens.
- **Pandit ji (text chat):** free.
- **Preeti (the helper on our site):** free.
- Palmistry, Numerology, Tarot, Marriage and Vastu guides are coming. Their prices will be announced.

## "Per started minute"

Every minute that you begin is charged as a full minute. If your talk runs 2 minutes and 20 seconds, that is 3 minutes, so 18 tokens.

## Very short talks are free

If the talk ends within **10 seconds**, you are not charged anything.

## You always see the price

- The price is shown **before you start**.
- You need at least **one minute's balance** (6 tokens for Meera) to begin.
- Your balance is visible during the talk.

## When your balance runs out

The talk ends politely. You are never charged for more than your wallet holds.
