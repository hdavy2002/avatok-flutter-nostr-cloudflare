## A summary to keep

After a talk, you can ask for a short summary or a fuller report of what the Guide said.

## Where it arrives

It is sent to you on **WhatsApp**, to the number you signed up with.

## Not received?

Check that the number on your account is right and that WhatsApp is installed. Then write to support (@) {{brand.domain}}.
