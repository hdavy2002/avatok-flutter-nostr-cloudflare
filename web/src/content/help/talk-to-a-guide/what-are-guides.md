## Talking, not typing

A Guide is a voice you can talk to, right in your browser or the app. You speak, the Guide listens and answers, a little like a phone call with someone who has studied one subject deeply.

## Every Guide has one subject

Each Guide has a name and covers one subject. **Astrology** is the first to launch. Palmistry, Numerology, Tarot, Marriage (kundli milan) and Vastu are planned for later.

## Clearly an AI

Our Guides are AI. We say so plainly, and you will never be asked to pretend otherwise. A Guide is a patient, knowledgeable listener, not a person, and not a replacement for one.

## What a Guide is for

A Guide gives tradition-based guidance to help you think things through. It does not promise outcomes. See [Guidance, not guarantees](/help/talk-to-a-guide/guidance-not-guarantee).
