## What a Guide offers

Guidance rooted in tradition, to help you reflect and decide with a calmer mind.

## What a Guide cannot do

- It cannot promise that anything will happen.
- It is **not medical advice**. For your health, see a doctor.
- It is **not legal advice**. For a legal matter, see a lawyer.
- It is **not financial advice**. For money decisions, see a qualified adviser.

## Your decisions are yours

Please do not make a big decision only because of what a Guide said. Use it as one voice among others you trust.

If a talk ever leaves you worried, you can end it at any time, or ask for a human. See [Talking to a human](/help/talk-to-a-guide/talk-to-a-human).
