## At a glance

- **Pandit ji** is for **typing**. He explains your kundli and what tradition says. **Free.**
- **Preeti** is the **helper on our site**. She answers questions about pujas, bookings and how things work. **Free.**
- **Meera** is for **talking by voice**, on astrology. **₹6 per started minute**, paid from your [wallet](/help/talk-to-a-guide/wallet-and-tokens).

All three are AI. We say so plainly.

## One memory

The three share one memory of you, if you allow it, so you never repeat yourself. See [Different Guides, one memory](/help/talk-to-a-guide/different-guides-share).

## Which one should I use?

- A quick question about the site or a booking: Preeti.
- To read about your chart in your own time: Pandit ji.
- To talk it through aloud: Meera.
