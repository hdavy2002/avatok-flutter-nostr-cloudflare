## Before you start

1. Choose the Guide whose subject fits your question.
2. Check the price shown on the screen. It is always shown before you begin. A voice talk with Meera needs at least one minute's balance (6 tokens) in your [wallet](/help/talk-to-a-guide/wallet-and-tokens).
3. If you want the Guide to remember your talks, tick the permission box. You are asked once, before your first talk. See [How memory works](/help/talk-to-a-guide/how-memory-works).
4. Share your birth details if the Guide needs them. See [Birth details](/help/talk-to-a-guide/birth-details).
5. Allow your microphone when your browser or phone asks.

## During the talk

Just speak naturally, in the language you are comfortable with. You can see your balance while you talk. If you end the talk within 10 seconds, you are not charged.

## When it ends

You can end the talk whenever you like. If your wallet balance runs out, the talk ends politely, and the Guide will not cut you off in the middle of a sentence.

If something goes wrong, see [Call problems](/help/talk-to-a-guide/call-problems).
