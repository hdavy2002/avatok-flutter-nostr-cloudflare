## Memory is your choice

Before your first talk there is a tick box. If you tick it, Guides remember your talks. If you do not, they do not.

## What is remembered

- What you talked about
- Worries you mentioned
- What the Guide suggested

So next time, the Guide already knows you, and you do not need to explain it all again.

## All Guides share it

Memory is shared across your Guides. What you told the Astrology Guide can help the Marriage Guide, for instance. See [Different Guides, one memory](/help/talk-to-a-guide/different-guides-share).

## You are in control

You can see and delete any memory, or ask a Guide to forget you. Memories are kept for up to **12 months**. See [See or delete your memories](/help/talk-to-a-guide/see-or-delete-memories).
