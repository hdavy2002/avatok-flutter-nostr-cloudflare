## The weekly guide

| Day | Planet (graha) | Deity commonly worshipped | Colours traditionally prescribed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sunday (Ravivar) | Surya | Surya Dev | Red, saffron, copper |
| Monday (Somvar) | Chandra | Shiva | White, silver |
| Tuesday (Mangalvar) | Mangal | Hanuman | Red |
| Wednesday (Budhvar) | Budh | Ganesha | Green |
| Thursday (Guruvar) | Guru (Brihaspati) | Vishnu | Yellow |
| Friday (Shukravar) | Shukra | Lakshmi, Santoshi Maa | White, light pink |
| Saturday (Shanivar) | Shani | Shani Dev, Hanuman | Black, dark blue |

Rahu and Ketu, the shadow planets, have no day of their own. Rahu is often grouped with Saturday (dark, smoky colours) and Ketu with Tuesday or Thursday (grey or mixed colours).

## How Pandit ji uses this

When your chart or question points to a planet, for example Shani during Sade Sati, he tells you the day, deity, mantra and colours tradition links to it, and shows designs whose **deity, symbol or print colours** match. See [Why was I suggested this design?](/help/talk-to-a-guide/why-this-design).

## Customs differ

These links are widely followed, but families, regions and sampradayas differ. If your family follows a different custom, follow your family, and tell Pandit ji so he can keep it in mind. See [How Guides remember you](/help/talk-to-a-guide/how-memory-works).
