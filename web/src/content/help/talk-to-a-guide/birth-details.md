## Why we ask

Your date, time and place of birth let the Guide read your chart. Without them, an astrology Guide can only speak in general terms.

## Asked once, shared by all Guides

You give your birth details one time. Every Guide on {{brand.name}} can use them, so you never repeat yourself.

## If you do not know your birth time

That is very common. The Guide works from your **moon chart** instead, and tells you which parts of the reading are **less certain** without an exact time. Ask your family if you can, since even an approximate time helps.

## Changing them

If you entered something wrong, you can correct it. Open your birth details from your profile, correct them and save. Or ask Pandit ji or Meera to note the correction.
