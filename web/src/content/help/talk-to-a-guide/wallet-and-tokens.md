## What is the wallet?

Your wallet holds **tokens**. **1 token = ₹1**. Voice talks with Meera are paid from it.

## What is free

Pandit ji (text chat) and Preeti (the helper on our site) are free. They never use your tokens.

## Adding money

Adding money to the wallet is **coming soon**. Until then there is no top-up.

## Starting a talk

You need at least **one minute's balance** to begin. For Meera that is 6 tokens. When your balance runs out during a talk, the talk ends politely.

## Seeing your balance

Your balance is shown before and during a talk.

## Good to know

- Tokens are for use on {{brand.name}} and cannot be sent to another person.
- Credits and refunds for call problems on our side come back as tokens. See [Paying and refunds](/help/talk-to-a-guide/paying-and-refunds).
