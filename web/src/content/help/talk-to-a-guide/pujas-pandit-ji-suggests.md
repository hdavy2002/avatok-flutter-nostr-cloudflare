## How a puja is chosen

1. Your chart is checked for the planets, periods and doshas that tradition gives remedies for.
2. Pandit ji looks at the pujas and havans **actually scheduled** on {{brand.name}}.
3. He suggests one only when tradition links it to what came up, and tells you why.

He also tells you what you can do yourself at home, such as a mantra, a fast or a simple offering, because tradition does not require a paid ritual.

## In the chat

A suggested puja appears as a card with its title and price. You can ignore it, ask him to explain, or open it. This is the same suggestion described in [When a Guide suggests a puja or havan](/help/talk-to-a-guide/puja-suggestions), with Pandit ji showing it as a card in writing.

## Booking and watching

Tap **Book my seat** on the card. Booking, payment, the live link and the sankalp in your name work as for any puja on {{brand.name}}. See [How to book](/help/booking-and-paying/how-to-book) and [Join a live show](/help/booking-and-paying/join-a-live-show).
