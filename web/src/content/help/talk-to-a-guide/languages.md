## Talk the way you normally talk

Guides understand and speak **Hindi, English and Hinglish**, and many more languages besides.

## Switching mid-talk

You do not need to pick a language first. If you start in English and feel more at ease in Hindi halfway through, just switch. The Guide follows you.

## Tips for a clear conversation

- Speak at your normal pace.
- Use headphones if there is background noise.
- Names of places and people are easiest to catch when spoken clearly.
