## See your memories

You can open the list of everything the Guides remember about you and read it. Ask Pandit ji or Meera "what do you remember about me?" and they will tell you.

## Delete one

Choose any single memory and delete it. It is gone and the Guides stop using it.

## Delete everything

Say **"forget me"** to a Guide, and everything is erased. You can start fresh at any time.

## How long memories last

Memories are kept for up to 12 months, then they go. You do not have to do anything for that to happen.
