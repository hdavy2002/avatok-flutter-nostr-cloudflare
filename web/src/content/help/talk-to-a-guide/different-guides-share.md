## One you, many Guides

Each Guide has its own subject, but they all know the same you.

## What is shared

- **Birth details**: asked once.
- **Memory**: if you ticked the permission box, what you discussed, your worries and what was suggested.

## Why it helps

If you spoke about a family worry with the Astrology Guide, the Marriage Guide can start from there.

## If you would rather not

You can delete any memory, or say "forget me" to erase everything. See [See or delete your memories](/help/talk-to-a-guide/see-or-delete-memories).
