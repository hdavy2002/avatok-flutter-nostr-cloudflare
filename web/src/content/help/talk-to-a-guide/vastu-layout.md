## Two easy ways

1. **Tell the Guide** which direction each room faces, for example "the kitchen is in the south-east".
2. **Share a photo of your floor plan**.

## Finding directions

A compass app on your phone works well. Stand in the middle of the room and note which way the main door faces.

## Good to know

The Guide can only work from what you share, so a clearer plan gives clearer guidance.
