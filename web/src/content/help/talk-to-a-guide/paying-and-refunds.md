## Paying

You pay by **UPI before the talk**. Your minutes are then ready to use.

## Refunds

[CONFIRM WITH OWNER] Minutes you have already used on a talk are **not refundable**. If a talk fails because of a **technical problem on our side**, the minutes are **credited back** to you.

For how refunds work on pujas and havans, see [Cancelling a booking and getting a refund](/help/billing/refunds).

## Asking for help

Write to support (@) {{brand.domain}} with your 12-digit UPI transaction number and what happened, and we will look into it.
