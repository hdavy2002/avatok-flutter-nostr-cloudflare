## Paying

Voice talks are paid from your **wallet**. 1 token = ₹1, and Meera uses 6 tokens for each started minute. See [What does a talk cost?](/help/talk-to-a-guide/pricing-minutes).

Adding money to the wallet is **coming soon**. Until then there is no top-up.

## Refunds

[CONFIRM WITH OWNER: refund rule for call minutes] Minutes used on a talk are charged as described on the cost page. If a talk fails because of a **technical problem on our side**, we aim to credit the tokens back to your wallet.

For how refunds work on pujas and havans, see [Cancelling a booking and getting a refund](/help/billing/refunds).

## Asking for help

Write to support (@) {{brand.domain}} with the date and time of the talk and what happened, and we will look into it.
