## The temple's pujari

The pujari who serves the temple performs your havan or puja. In many cases his family has cared for that temple for generations. He says the **sankalp** for each devotee taking part, just as he would with you seated beside him.

## The {{brand.name}} crew

Our small team makes the long trip into the mountains to every temple. We set up next to the altar, stream the ritual live whenever the mountain network allows, and always film it from start to finish so the video is yours to keep. We also pack dry prasad from the temple and dispatch it the same day.

## Why this matters

Our partner temples lie well away from the pilgrim routes, and the people who look after them get through hard winters on very little. Many still climb up with food for the sadhus meditating in the caves above. Your booking brings them a fair, dependable income and a way to share their devotion with families everywhere.
