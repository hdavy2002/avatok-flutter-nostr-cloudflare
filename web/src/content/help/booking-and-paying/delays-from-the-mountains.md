## Our team goes to the temple for you

Our team travels to the temple with a camera crew and works alongside the temple's own priest. We set up live streaming at the altar so you can watch your puja or havan from home as it happens. See the temples we work with on [Our temples](/temples).

## When the weather gets in the way

Many of our temples are in the mountains of Uttarakhand and Himachal. Landslides, snowstorms and heavy rain are part of life there, and they can cut the network for hours or even days.

If that happens during your puja, do not worry. Our crew films the whole ritual on camera regardless. As soon as they are back in the studio, they upload your video and we send you the link to watch or download it.

**We will tell you about any delay on WhatsApp and by email**, so you are never left wondering.

## Prasad can be delayed too

The same weather can close mountain roads. Your prasad is packed and sent as soon as the road and the courier allow, so it may arrive later than usual. Please keep rain, snow, landslides and other natural events in mind when you book.

We send only dry, sealed prasad such as mishri, elaichi dana and dry fruits, so a delay does not spoil it.

## Charawa stays with the deity

What you offer at the altar, such as chadhava, vastra, coconut, flowers or dakshina, is given to the deity during your puja. By tradition it stays at the temple. Charawa is not returned to you and cannot be refunded once the puja has been performed.
