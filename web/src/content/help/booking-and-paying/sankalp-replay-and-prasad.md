## Your sankalp

In our tradition, the sankalp is what ties a ritual to a person. When you book, you share your full name, your gotra, a few words about your wish, and — if you'd like — the names of family members to include. Our priest speaks them at the altar, on camera, so you hear your name as it happens.

The yajman has never had to stand at the altar; temples have performed pujas for absent devotees for generations. Watching live is your participation.

## Gotra

Your gotra is your family lineage. It's optional — if you don't know yours, priests use **"Kashyap"**, as is customary.

## Replay

Missed the live stream? Your sankalp was still performed, and the replay is available for **7 days**.

## Prasad

Choose prasad delivery at checkout and add a delivery address. We post prasad to your door after the ritual.
