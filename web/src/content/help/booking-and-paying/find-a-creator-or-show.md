## Start at the marketplace

Everything bookable on avaTOK — live streams, one-on-one time, booked expert sessions — is listed at [avatok.ai/marketplace](/marketplace). You do not need to sign in to look around: browsing, searching and opening a listing all work for a visitor. You only need to identify yourself (with your email) when you actually book a seat.

## The three groups

The marketplace organises every listing into three groups, and only these three:

- **India Goes Live** — live streaming, the "pawri" zone. Link: [/marketplace?group=india_goes_live](/marketplace?group=india_goes_live)
- **Find Your People** — one-on-one, real company. Link: [/marketplace?group=find_your_people](/marketplace?group=find_your_people)
- **Book Their Time** — experts you book by the slot, the "gyaan desk". Link: [/marketplace?group=book_their_time](/marketplace?group=book_their_time)

Tap a group tile on the marketplace itself, or open one of the links above directly — either way you land on that group's listings with the tile you picked already selected. Inside a group, each listing also carries a sub-category (a "blip", like a particular topic or skill), and the filter rail lets you narrow further by that, by price or by language.

A group only shows up as a tile when it actually has something listed in it — an empty group is hidden rather than shown as a dead end.

## Live now and search

A "Live now" strip appears above the listings whenever a stream is actually live at that moment, so you can jump straight into something happening right now instead of scrolling for it. The search box in the hero strip works on plain text — creator name, listing title, topic — and turns your query into a shareable URL, so you can send a friend the exact search you ran rather than describing it.

## Opening a listing

Tapping any card takes you to that listing's own page, which shows what it actually is (a live event, a one-on-one consult, or a booked session), who is hosting it, when it runs and in what timezone, and the price. From there, "Book" or "Get your ticket" takes you into checkout — see [Booking a 1:1 session](/help/booking-and-paying/book-a-1-1-session) and [Joining a live show](/help/booking-and-paying/join-a-live-show) for what happens next.

## Creator profiles

Every creator has their own page (reached from their name or avatar on a listing) showing who they are and what else they have listed. It is the fastest way to see everything one creator is currently offering, across all three groups, in one place.

## A note on what you will see

avaTOK is a young marketplace, so at any given moment a group may have very few listings, or none — that is not a bug, it is how many creators have published so far. If a group is empty it simply will not appear as a tile that day.
