## The full policy

This is the plain-English version for creators. The binding terms are in [Payouts & Withdrawals](/payouts) — that page is authoritative if anything here differs from it.

## Payouts are still in testing

avaTOK's payment gateway application is still under review, so **withdrawals are limited to invited users, processed manually, and may be delayed or paused**. Don't treat avaTOK as a source of income during this period — see [Payouts & Withdrawals §1](/payouts).

## Who can withdraw

Any account holder with an **unused token balance** — tokens you haven't spent — can request a withdrawal, as long as your account is in good standing and withdrawal is permitted where you live. Tokens already spent on a feature or a booking, and free or bonus tokens, can't be withdrawn. Withdrawal can be unavailable while an account is suspended, under review, or has an open payment dispute. See [Payouts & Withdrawals §2](/payouts).

## UPI payouts in India

For India, payouts go out in INR to a **verified UPI address linked to your own Indian bank account** — the UPI address and the account behind it must belong to you, and avaTOK can decline a payout to someone else's account. Because a token is fixed at ₹1, there's no currency conversion: 500 unused tokens is ₹500 before the transfer fee and any tax. Double-check the UPI address before you submit it — a payout sent to a valid but wrong address you supplied may not be recoverable. See [Payouts & Withdrawals §3](/payouts).

Outside India, payouts aren't available while avaTOK is testing.

## Verification comes first

Before you can request a withdrawal, you need to accept the creator agreement, complete document (ID) verification, and add a UPI address, which the avaTOK team verifies. After you add or change a UPI address there's a short waiting period before it can be used, and during testing each request is reviewed and paid manually. A payout can be held or declined until these checks are complete — see [Payouts & Withdrawals §5](/payouts).

## Minimum balance

A minimum withdrawable balance may apply. Where it does, it's shown to you right on the withdrawal screen — check there for the current figure rather than relying on a number from elsewhere. See [Payouts & Withdrawals §8](/payouts).

## Transfer fee, GST, and what actually lands in your account

The [Payouts & Withdrawals](/payouts) policy (§6) allows for a transfer fee, plus GST where it applies in India, and your own bank may add a charge. During testing the withdrawal request doesn't yet show a fee or tax breakdown, so if you want to know what will be deducted, ask support@avatok.ai before you withdraw. See also [GST and tax on your payouts](/help/billing/gst-and-tax).

## Timelines

Any timeframe shown to you is an **estimate, not a guarantee**. Verification, avaTOK's payment partners, and your own bank all affect it, and public holidays or banking cut-offs can add delay. See [Payouts & Withdrawals §9](/payouts).

## If a payout fails or is returned

If a payout fails or bounces back — an inactive UPI address, a closed bank account, mismatched details — the amount is normally returned to your token balance so you can try again with corrected details. A fee already charged on the failed attempt may not be recoverable, and repeated failures may mean re-verifying your payout account. See [Payouts & Withdrawals §10](/payouts).

## Holds and declines

A payout can be delayed, held, reduced, or declined where avaTOK reasonably suspects fraud, money laundering, sanctions exposure, or a breach of the [Terms of Service](/terms) — or where law or a payment partner requires it. Amounts you owe, such as a reversed transaction, can also be set off against a pending payout. See [Payouts & Withdrawals §11](/payouts).
