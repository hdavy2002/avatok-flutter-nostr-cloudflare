## The full policy

This page is the plain-English version. The binding policy is [Refunds & Cancellations](/refunds); always defer to that page if anything here seems to disagree with it.

## What you see is what you pay

The price on an event is the full price you pay — no tokens, wallets or extra charges.

## Cancelling

Refunds are not automatic, and there are none for cancelling late or for missing the event. How much notice you need depends on what you booked:

- **Havan & Puja** — cancel at least **24 hours before the scheduled start** for a **full refund**. By then our team has made arrangements and often travelled, the priest has given his time and travelled to perform it, and the samagri, flowers and puja materials have already been bought.
- **Satsang, Sermon & Meditation** — cancel at least **3 days before the scheduled start** for a **full refund**. A hall or venue has been booked and the preacher or teacher has often travelled from far away, which is why these need more notice.

## Requesting a refund

Refunds are not automatic. Email **support (@) saathum.com** or use the [contact form](/contact) with:

1. Your full name, and the email or phone number you booked with.
2. The event and its scheduled date and time.
3. Your **12-digit UPI transaction number** (UTR / UPI reference number).

We use the UPI transaction number to locate your payment, so we can't process a refund without it. You'll find it in your UPI app's transaction history or in your bank's payment SMS.

Once we've confirmed the cancellation was within your event's notice period, we refund the full amount to the UPI account you paid from and let you know by email.

## If we cancel

If we cancel your event or can't perform it, you get a full refund regardless of notice — send us a request the same way, with your UPI transaction number.

## No automatic refunds for missed attendance

For a havan or puja, your sankalp is still performed even if you couldn't join, so the booking isn't refunded. For a satsang, sermon or meditation session, the event still happens at its scheduled time, so a missed session isn't refunded either. Your video is sent to you when it finishes.

## Wrong or duplicate payments

Email support (@) saathum.com with the UPI transaction number for each payment. Please contact us before raising a dispute with your bank — it's faster.
