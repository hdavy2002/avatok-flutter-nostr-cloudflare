Sometimes a charge may look wrong. If it really is a mistake on our side, we put the money back. Here is how to tell, and how to report it.

## What counts as a billing error

These are billing errors:

- You were **charged for a call that was never made**.
- You were **charged twice**.

You should pay nothing when a call is only ringing, or when the influencer declines, cancels or does not answer. A call is made once we have rung both of you and joined the call.

If a joined call breaks in the first minute, we charge ₹1 and return the rest. That is the rule, not a mistake.

## What is not a billing error

If a call was made and you simply did not enjoy it, that is not a billing error. We do not refund calls that were made. Read [why there are no refunds](/help/wallet-and-payments/no-refunds) to understand why.

Also remember that you pay by the minute you speak, at the influencer's per-minute rate shown before you call. If the charge matches that rate and the connected minutes, it is correct billing. If we find a mistake, the wrong amount is put back in your wallet and linked to that call.

## How to report it

Check your wallet history first. It shows each charge with date, duration and amount. Then write to support (@) {{brand.domain}} with:

1. The **call ID**
2. The **date and time** of the call
3. The **wrong amount**
4. The **transaction reference**, if it is a top-up problem

A clear report helps us fix it faster.

## What happens next

We look at it. If it is a real billing error, **the wrong amount is put back in your wallet**. It is not a refund. It is just fixing our mistake.

Rohit sees two charges of the same amount for one 3-minute call. He writes to support with the call ID, the time and the amount. The extra charge goes back to his wallet.

## Stay safe

**Never send your OTP, UPI PIN, Aadhaar number or card details** in your message. We will never ask for them. Not on a call, WhatsApp or email.

If you feel something is unfair, you can also use the [grievance page](/grievance). See the [wallet terms](/wallet-terms) for the full rules.
