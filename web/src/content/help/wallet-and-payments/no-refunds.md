We do not refund calls that were made. We know this can feel hard, so here is the rule in plain words.

## Why no refunds for calls that were made?

**You are in control of every call.**

- You choose the influencer.
- You see the rate before you call.
- You can hang up any second.

You pay only for the minutes you spoke. We are not responsible for how a particular call feels, so we cannot refund a call just because it was not what you hoped for.

## When you pay nothing

A call is made once we have rung both of you and joined the call. Until then, nothing is charged.

- If the influencer declines, cancels or does not answer, you pay nothing.
- A call that fails before we join you costs nothing.

## The first-minute rule

If a joined call breaks within the first minute, we **charge ₹1 and return the rest** to your wallet.

## Booked calls

A booked call holds 10 minutes of the influencer's rate in your wallet.

- You can cancel **free until 1 hour before** the booked time. The hold comes back to your wallet.
- If the influencer does not pick up at the booked time, the **full hold comes back** to your wallet.
- If the influencer cancels, it **always comes back**.
- If you cancel later than 1 hour before, the hold is kept.

## All refunds go to your wallet

Every refund goes back to your wallet. We never send a refund to your card or UPI. If you want the money in your bank, use the Withdraw button. See [withdraw your wallet money](/help/wallet-and-payments/withdraw-your-balance).

## An example

Amit calls his favourite cricket YouTuber to just chat. After four minutes, he feels the talk is not fun. It was not a rule break. It just did not click.

- Amit hangs up. He pays only for the four minutes he talked.
- There is **no refund** for those minutes.
- He can try a different influencer next time, and rate the influencer honestly.

## What you can do instead

- **Hang up early.** If it is not working, you can end the call at any time.
- **Try someone else.** Filter by language, price and online now.
- **Rate the influencer.** Your rating helps other callers.
- **Report a rule break.** If the influencer was rude, asked for money, or broke a rule, [report them](/report). Our team will look at it.

## Charged by mistake?

If you were charged twice, or charged for a call that was never made, that is a billing error. It is not a refund. The wrong amount is put back in your wallet. Read [billing errors](/help/wallet-and-payments/billing-errors).
