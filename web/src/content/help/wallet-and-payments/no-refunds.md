We do not give refunds for calls that connected. We know this can feel hard, so here is the reason, in plain words.

## Why no refunds?

**You are in control of every call.**

- You choose the host.
- You see the price before you call.
- You can hang up any second.

Charging starts only when you are both connected, and only for the minutes you actually talked. We are not responsible for how a particular call feels, so we cannot refund a call just because it was not what you hoped.

## An example

Amit calls a host to just chat. After four minutes, he feels the talk is not fun. It was not a rule break. It just did not click.

- Amit hangs up. He pays only for the minutes he talked.
- There is **no refund** for those minutes.
- He can try a different host who fits his mood better.
- He can rate the host honestly, so others know.

That is fine. Not every conversation is a match, just like in real life.

## What you can do instead

- **Hang up early.** If it is not working, you can end the call at any time.
- **Try someone else.** Filter by mood, language, price and online now.
- **Rate the host.** Your rating helps other callers.
- **Report a rule break.** If the host was rude, asked for money, or broke a rule, [report them](/report). Our team will look at it.

## When money does go back

You pay nothing if the call never connects. Ringing, a host declining, or a timeout all cost ₹0.

If you were charged for a call that never connected, or charged twice, that is a billing error. It is not a refund. The wrong amount is put back in your wallet. Read [billing errors](/help/wallet-and-payments/billing-errors) to learn how to report it.

## Your unused balance

Money you have not used is always yours. You can [withdraw it](/help/wallet-and-payments/withdraw-your-balance) to your own UPI ID.
