Some influencers sell T-shirts with their own art. You can buy one in the app or on the website.

## How to buy

1. Open an influencer's page and tap **Shop**.
2. Pick a T-shirt and your size.
3. Check the price. It is **all-in**: delivery in India is free, and there is no extra line at checkout.
4. Add your delivery address and tick the terms and refund policy.
5. Pay **from your wallet** or **online**.

Your wallet pays from money you added. Free test credit cannot be used for merch.

## After you pay

Each order is checked by our team, then printed to order. This takes a little time. You can follow it in **My orders**. We send you the courier and tracking link by WhatsApp and email.

The influencer never sees your phone number, email, full address or payment details.

## Returns and problems

T-shirts are printed just for you, so **we cannot take returns**. If the item is wrong or damaged, tell us within **48 hours of delivery** and send a photo. We will replace it or refund you. A refund goes back the way you paid: to your wallet if you paid from your wallet.

If an order cannot be made, we refund you in full.

Read the [refund policy](/refunds) and [wallet terms](/wallet-terms) for the full rules.
