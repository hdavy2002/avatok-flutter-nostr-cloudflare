Some influencers sell T-shirts with their own art. You can buy one in the app or on the website.

## How to buy

1. Open an influencer's page and find their **T-shirts**.
2. Pick a T-shirt, a colour and your size.
3. Look at the price. It is **all-in**: delivery anywhere in India is free, and there is no extra GST or delivery line.
4. Add your delivery address.
5. Tick the **Terms** and the **refund policy**.
6. Pay.

## How you can pay

- **Your wallet**. It pays only from money you added. **Free test credit cannot be used for merch.**
- **UPI, card or net banking**, on the secure payment page.

## After you pay

Each order moves through these steps: **Placed**, **Approved**, **Printing**, **Shipped** and **Delivered**. Our team checks every paid order before it is printed, and each T-shirt is printed just for you, so give it some time.

When it ships we send the courier name and a tracking link to you on **WhatsApp and email**. You can also follow it any time in **My orders** in the dashboard and in the app. See [Track your merch order](/help/wallet-and-payments/track-your-merch-order).

The influencer never sees your phone number, email, full address or payment details. They see only your first name and your city and state.

## Returns and problems

T-shirts are printed for you, so there are **no returns or exchanges**. If the T-shirt is wrong or damaged, tell us **within 48 hours of delivery** with a photo, and we will replace it or refund you. See [Wrong or damaged T-shirt](/help/wallet-and-payments/merch-wrong-or-damaged-item).

Refunds go back **the way you paid**: to your wallet if you paid from your wallet, and to your payment method if you paid online. If we cannot make your order, we refund you in full.

Read the [refund policy](/refunds) and the [wallet terms](/wallet-terms) for the full rules.
