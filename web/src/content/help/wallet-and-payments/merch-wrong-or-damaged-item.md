We are sorry if your T-shirt did not arrive right. Here is what to do.

## No returns or exchanges

Every T-shirt is printed just for you, so we **cannot take returns or exchanges**, for example if you ordered the wrong size or changed your mind.

## Wrong or damaged item

If you got the wrong T-shirt, or it is damaged or the print is faulty:

1. Report it **within 48 hours of delivery**. Use the report option on the order in **My orders**.
2. Say whether it is **wrong** or **damaged**, and tell us what happened.
3. Add a **clear photo**.

Our team checks it. If it is our mistake, we will **replace** the T-shirt or **refund** you.

## Where the refund goes

A refund goes back **the way you paid**. Paid from your wallet: it comes back to your wallet. Paid online with UPI, card or net banking: it goes back to that payment method.

If we cannot make your order at all, you get a full refund too.

See the [refund policy](/refunds), and [how to track your order](/help/wallet-and-payments/track-your-merch-order).
