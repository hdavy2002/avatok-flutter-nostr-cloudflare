Money in your wallet is yours. Anyone can take it out. This page shows you how.

## Your wallet money is yours

Fans and influencers use the same **Withdraw** button, on the website and in the app. We send the money to **your own UPI ID**. We never take your money as a penalty, even if something goes wrong with your account.

## How to withdraw

1. Sign in and open **Wallet**.
2. Open the **Withdrawal setup** card and add your UPI ID, in your own name.
3. Do the one-time ID check, if it is your first time.
4. Tap **Withdraw to UPI**, type the amount in whole rupees and confirm.

We take **no fee**. You can ask for up to **2 withdrawals a week**. Withdrawals go **only to UPI**. We do not use a bank account.

The ID check is done **once**. After your first paid withdrawal you will not be asked again.

## Follow your request

Each request shows one of these:

- **Waiting for our check**. You can **cancel** it now, and the money stays in your wallet.
- **Approved**.
- **Paid**, with the bank reference (UTR) of the transfer.
- **Not paid**, with the reason. The money goes back to your wallet.

## What cannot be withdrawn

- **Test credits.** They can be spent on calls, but they cannot be withdrawn.
- **Influencer earnings still in the 7-day hold.** Influencers can withdraw earnings 7 days after the call.

## Refunds are different

A refund (for example, a booked call the influencer cancelled) goes back to your **wallet**, never straight to your card or UPI. To get it into your bank, use Withdraw. See [do you refund calls?](/help/wallet-and-payments/no-refunds).

## When to withdraw

- **When you stop using the service.** Take out what is left so nothing stays behind.
- **Before you delete your account.** See [delete your account](/help/account-and-help/delete-your-account).
- **After a ban.** You cannot make calls, but you can still sign in and use Withdraw.

Influencers can also read [how earnings reach you](/help/hosts/getting-paid).

## Stay safe

We will **never** ask for your OTP, UPI PIN, password or bank details. If someone says they will "help you withdraw" and asks for these, it is a scam. Do not share them. You can [report it](/report).
