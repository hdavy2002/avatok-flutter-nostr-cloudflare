Every call, whether you call now, wait in the [queue](/help/calling/join-the-queue) or [book a time](/help/calling/book-a-time), uses the same simple money rules.

## You pay for the minutes you speak

You pay by the minute, at the influencer's per-minute rate. You see the rate on her page before you call. You pay nothing while the phone is ringing, if she declines, if she misses the call, or if you cancel.

## 5 minutes at a time

While you talk, we set aside the cost of the **next 5 minutes** from your wallet. This way your call is not cut by surprise. Money that is set aside but not used **comes back to your wallet** when the call ends.

To start a call you need at least **₹20**, or the cost of 5 minutes at her rate if that is more.

## When your wallet is running low

If your wallet cannot cover the next 5 minutes, **only you** hear a short message. It tells you about how many minutes are left and asks you to top up while you speak. The influencer never hears why. She only hears that the call will end in one minute.

You can top up **in the app or on the website** while you keep talking. As soon as the money arrives, the call continues. You hear "Your call continues."

## The longest call

A call can run for up to **5 hours**.

## If a call breaks

If a joined call breaks in the **first minute**, we charge **₹1** and the rest goes back to your wallet. If the influencer declines, does not answer, or a booked call is cancelled, you pay **₹0**.

## Booked calls

A booking holds the cost of the **first 15 minutes** when you book. The rest is charged 5 minutes at a time while you talk. Unused money comes back. See [Book a time](/help/calling/book-a-time).

## Refunds go to your wallet

When money is given back to you, it goes to your wallet, never to your card or UPI. To get it into your bank, [withdraw it](/help/wallet-and-payments/withdraw-your-balance).
