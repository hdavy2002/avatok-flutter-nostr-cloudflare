Every call, whether you call now, wait in a queue or book a time, uses the same simple money rules.

## 5 minutes ahead

While you talk, we set aside the cost of the **next 5 minutes** from your wallet, so your call is never cut by surprise. You are charged only for the **minutes you actually speak**, counted per second. Whatever was set aside and not used goes back to your wallet when the call ends.

## When your wallet is running low

If your wallet cannot cover the next 5 minutes, **only you** hear a message: "Your wallet balance has run out. This call will end in about X minutes. Please top up your wallet while you are speaking." The influencer hears only that the call will end in one minute, never the reason.

On the website you also see a **Top up now — your call continues** button. As soon as the money arrives, the call is extended and you hear "Thanks — your wallet is topped up. Your call continues."

## The longest call

A call can run for up to **5 hours**.

## Booked calls

A booking holds the cost of the **first 15 minutes** when you book. The rest is charged 5 minutes at a time while you talk, like any other call. If you cancel before the call, the full hold comes back.
