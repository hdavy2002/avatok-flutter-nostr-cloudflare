Your wallet decides how long you can talk. Here is what to expect, so a call never ends as a surprise.

## The 2-minute minimum

To start a call, you need balance for **at least 2 minutes** at that host's rate.

- Host at ₹5 per minute: you need ₹10.
- Host at ₹10 per minute: you need ₹20.
- Host at ₹20 per minute: you need ₹40.

If you have less, add money first. See [top up your wallet](/help/wallet-and-payments/top-up-your-wallet).

## How long can I talk?

A call lasts as many **whole minutes your balance can buy**, up to **60 minutes** per call.

- Rohit has ₹50 and calls a host at ₹10 per minute. His balance buys 5 minutes.
- Meera has ₹700 and calls a host at ₹5 per minute. Her balance could buy 140 minutes, but one call is limited to 60 minutes.

## The warning and the automatic end

About **60 seconds before** your limit, you hear a warning. Then the call ends by itself when the time is up. This can be because your balance is finished or because you reached 60 minutes.

**Your balance never goes below zero.** You will never owe us money.

## After the call ends

You can start a new call if you add more money and the host is still online. If you reached the 60-minute limit, you can call again if you want to keep talking.

## How billing works

You pay per **started minute** of connected time. For example:

- 61 seconds at ₹5 per minute is 2 minutes, so ₹10.
- 3 minutes 20 seconds at ₹10 per minute is 4 minutes, so ₹40.

You pay only when both of you are connected. Ringing or a call that never connects costs ₹0.

## Tips

- Add a little extra before a long chat.
- Check the host's price before you call. It is shown on their profile.
- Look at your wallet history any time to see all charges.

If you think a charge is wrong, read [billing errors](/help/wallet-and-payments/billing-errors). For full rules, see the [wallet terms](/wallet-terms).
