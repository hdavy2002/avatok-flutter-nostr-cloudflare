Your wallet decides how long you can talk. Here is what to expect, so a call never ends as a surprise.

## The minimum balance

To start a call, you need enough money in your wallet to cover the start of the call at the influencer's rate. The app tells you if you need to add more.

If you have less, add money first. See [top up your wallet](/help/wallet-and-payments/top-up-your-wallet).

## How long can I talk?

There is **no fixed time limit**. A call goes on until either person hangs up or your wallet money is used up.

- If an influencer's rate is ₹10 a minute (only an example), Rohit with ₹100 in his wallet can talk for about 10 minutes.
- The more money you add, the longer you can talk.

## The warning and the automatic end

About **one minute before** your wallet money ends, you hear "one minute left". If you want to keep talking, you cannot add money in the middle of the call, so the call ends by itself when the money is finished.

**Your balance never goes below zero.** You will never owe us money.

## After the call ends

You can start a new call if you add more money and the influencer is still online. A new call is charged at the influencer's rate again.

## How billing works

You pay for the exact seconds you are connected, at the influencer's per-minute rate.

You pay only when both of you are connected. Ringing or a call that never connects costs ₹0.

## Tips

- Add a little extra before a long chat.
- Check the influencer's rate before you call.
- Look at your wallet history any time to see all charges.

If you think a charge is wrong, read [billing errors](/help/wallet-and-payments/billing-errors). For full rules, see the [wallet terms](/wallet-terms).
