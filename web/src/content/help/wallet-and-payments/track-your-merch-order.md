Your T-shirt is printed after you order, so it does not leave the same day. Here is how to follow it.

## Where to look

Open **My orders** in your dashboard on the website or in the app. Tap an order to see where it is.

## The steps

1. **Placed**: your payment is done.
2. **Approved**: our team checked your order.
3. **Printing**: your T-shirt is being printed.
4. **Shipped**: it is with the courier. You now see the **courier name and a tracking link**.
5. **Delivered**: it reached you.

We send you a **WhatsApp message and an email** at each step, so you do not need to keep checking.

## If something is wrong

- If the T-shirt arrives wrong or damaged, read [Wrong or damaged T-shirt](/help/wallet-and-payments/merch-wrong-or-damaged-item). You must tell us within 48 hours of delivery.
- If we could not make your order, we refund you in full, the way you paid.
- For anything else, write to support with your order number. Never send an OTP, UPI PIN or card details.
