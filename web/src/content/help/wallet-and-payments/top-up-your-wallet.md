On {{brand.name}}, you pay for calls from a prepaid talk-time wallet. You add money first, then you talk. It is simple.

## What is the wallet?

The wallet holds your talk-time money in rupees. When you call an influencer, the cost of the call is taken from it minute by minute. You can only use what you have added, so there are no surprise bills. Joining is free. There is no subscription.

## How to add money

1. Open your wallet.
2. Type how much to add. You can add **any whole amount from ₹10 to ₹5,000**.
3. Pay on the secure payment page.

Money is added only after your payment is confirmed, and only once.

## GST and what reaches your wallet

18% GST is deducted from the amount you pay, so your wallet gets the amount divided by 1.18. Before you pay, you see three things: the total you pay, your GST being deducted, and the amount added to your wallet. Your receipt shows the same three.

| You pay | GST | Added to wallet |
|---|---|---|
| ₹120 | ₹18.31 | ₹101.69 |
| ₹240 | ₹36.61 | ₹203.39 |
| ₹600 | ₹91.53 | ₹508.47 |
| ₹1,200 | ₹183.05 | ₹1,016.95 |

No GST is charged again when you spend wallet money on calls.

## How much should I add?

Each influencer sets their own per-minute rate, and you see it before you call. You need **at least ₹20**, or the cost of 5 minutes at the influencer's rate if that is more, to start a call.

Rohit from Pune wants a quick chat. He pays ₹120, so ₹101.69 goes into his wallet. That is more than the ₹20 he needs to start, so he can call.

## Your wallet history

Every addition and every call charge shows in your wallet history. For each line you can see the date, the duration and the amount. If you think there is a mistake, read [billing errors](/help/wallet-and-payments/billing-errors).

## Payment receipts

Open **Wallet**, then **Payment receipts**, and tap **View** to open the receipt for a top-up. It shows what you paid, your GST that was deducted and what reached your wallet.

## Good to know

- A call is made once we have rung both of you and joined the call. Ringing, an influencer declining, cancelling or not answering costs ₹0.
- Refunds always go back to your wallet. You can take wallet money out with the [Withdraw button](/help/wallet-and-payments/withdraw-your-balance).
- Read the full [wallet terms](/wallet-terms) if you want every detail.

## Stay safe

We will **never** ask you for your OTP, UPI PIN, password or bank details. Not on a call, WhatsApp or email. Enter your UPI PIN only inside your own UPI app when you pay. Never tell it to anyone.
