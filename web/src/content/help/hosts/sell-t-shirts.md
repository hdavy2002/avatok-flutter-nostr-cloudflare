You can sell T-shirts, hoodies, mugs, bottles, phone cases, posters and more with your own art on {{brand.name}}. We print each one when a fan orders it and deliver it to them, so you never hold stock or post anything yourself.

## Make a product

1. Open **Merch** in your dashboard or in the app.
2. Upload your art and tick **I own this art**.
3. Pick a T-shirt. Choose the **front or the back**, the **colours** and the **sizes** (S to 5XL).
4. Place your art on the shirt. A **quality meter** tells you if the art is too small or blurry to print well, and it will not let a blurry print go through. If a picture is too blurry at the size you want, tap **Make it sharper (free)**: we make a bigger, sharper copy of it (up to 10 a day). We make AI pictures extra large so they can fill the whole front of a T-shirt.
5. Add your **markup**. The lowest markup is **₹10** (currently).
6. Send it for review.

## How the price works

For every size there is a **base price**. It is made of two parts that you see separately, size by size, while you set up the product:

- **Printing (incl. GST)**: the cost of printing the T-shirt, with GST already included.
- **Shipping**: delivery to the fan anywhere in India.

**Base price = printing (incl. GST) + shipping.** For example, for a Premium Oversized tee in black with a small print: printing incl. GST ₹393.75 + shipping ₹50 = base ₹444 (rounded up). Add a ₹200 markup and the fan pays **₹644 incl. 5% GST & delivery** (clothing is taxed at 5%; other items at their own rate), and you earn ₹120.

The fan pays **base price + your markup** as one all-in price, labelled **incl. GST & delivery**: 5% on clothing up to ₹2,500, and other items at their own GST rate. Delivery anywhere in India is free for the fan, and nothing is added at checkout. Clothing is capped at **₹2,500**, so the 5% GST rate applies; the markup box stops you going over it. Bigger sizes can have a higher base price, so the fan price can differ by size.

## Review

Every new product is reviewed by our team before it goes on sale. This is because of copyright and brand-logo worries, so please only use art that is yours. Once it is approved it shows on your public page.

Every paid order is also checked by our team before it is printed. In the rare case that the printing cost has gone up a lot since you set the price, we may cancel that order and give the fan a full refund. You will be told to raise your price.

## What you earn

You earn a **design royalty of 60% of your markup** (currently), so a ₹200 markup earns you ₹120. We are the seller of every item: we collect the fan's payment, pay the GST and send the fan a GST invoice. That never reduces your royalty.

**You do not need a GST number to start.** There is an optional GSTIN field if you want to add one.

Your merch earnings can be withdrawn **7 days after the order is delivered** (currently), with the same Withdraw button as your call earnings. An order that is refunded earns nothing. Read more in [Your merch orders and earnings](/help/hosts/merch-orders-and-earnings) and [Getting paid](/help/hosts/getting-paid).
