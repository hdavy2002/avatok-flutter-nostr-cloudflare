You can sell T-shirts with your own art. We print each one to order and deliver it, so you hold no stock.

## Create a product

1. Open **Merch** in your dashboard or the app.
2. Upload your art and tick **I own this art**.
3. Pick a T-shirt and place the art on it.
4. Set your **markup**. The lowest markup is ₹10.
5. Publish it for review.

The price fans see is our **base price** plus your markup. Delivery in India is free.

## Review

Every new product is reviewed by our team before it appears on your page. Every paid order is checked too, before it is printed.

## What you earn

You earn **60% of your markup**. We keep 40%. Our GST and payment fees come out of our share, never yours.

Your merch money can be withdrawn **7 days after delivery**, with the same Withdraw button as your call earnings. An order that is refunded earns nothing. See [Getting paid](/help/hosts/getting-paid).

## Orders

Open your orders to see each one and its tracking. For privacy you see only the buyer's first name, city, state and a blurred address line.
