You can sell T-shirts with your own art on {{brand.name}}. We print each one when a fan orders it and deliver it to them, so you never hold stock or post anything yourself.

## Make a product

1. Open **Merch** in your dashboard or in the app.
2. Upload your art and tick **I own this art**.
3. Pick a T-shirt. Choose the **front or the back**, the **colours** and the **sizes** (S to 5XL).
4. Place your art on the shirt. A **quality meter** tells you if the art is too small or blurry to print well, and it will not let a blurry print go through.
5. Add your **markup**. The lowest markup is **₹10** (currently).
6. Send it for review.

## How the price works

For every size there is a **base price**. It covers the printing and the delivery. You see it while you set up the product.

The fan pays **base price + your markup**, all in. Delivery anywhere in India is free, and there is no extra GST or delivery line at checkout. Bigger sizes can have a higher base price, so the fan price can differ by size.

## Review

Every new product is reviewed by our team before it goes on sale. This is because of copyright and brand-logo worries, so please only use art that is yours. Once it is approved it shows on your public page.

Every paid order is also checked by our team before it is printed. In the rare case that the printing cost has gone up a lot since you set the price, we may cancel that order and give the fan a full refund. You will be told to raise your price.

## What you earn

You keep **60% of your markup** (currently). We keep 40%, and we pay the GST and payment fees out of our share. They never reduce what you earn.

Your merch earnings can be withdrawn **7 days after the order is delivered** (currently), with the same Withdraw button as your call earnings. An order that is refunded earns nothing. Read more in [Your merch orders and earnings](/help/hosts/merch-orders-and-earnings) and [Getting paid](/help/hosts/getting-paid).
