{{brand.name}} connects people to their favourite social media influencers (YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and more) over a private phone call. As an influencer, you talk to your fans on a normal phone call and earn money for every minute. You can do it from home, whenever you feel like it.

## Who can be an influencer

- You must be **18 years or older**.
- For now, we only take on **influencers based in India**, with their own Indian mobile number.
- You do not need a YouTube or Instagram account. If you have one, you can prove it is yours. This is called **Social media verification**, and it is optional.
- No social media account? Then you verify with your **Aadhaar** instead. Aadhaar is also needed if you pick **Listener**, **Prayer**, **Virtual friend** or **Women-to-Women**.
- Individuals are welcome to chat, keep company and share their day. Sexual or adult talk is never allowed.
- A Women-to-Women channel needs an Aadhaar that shows female.
- Every creator can choose to take calls only from video-KYC customers, for free. We never share any customer’s details with anyone.

Influencers on {{brand.name}} are not doctors, lawyers, counsellors, CAs or financial advisors. This is a place to talk to your fans, not to give professional advice.

## Steps to start

Becoming an influencer happens **in our app**. The website shows a button to download it.

1. **Create your account.** It is free.
2. **Pick your categories.** Choose one or more, like Cooking, Travel or Music.
3. **Social media verification (optional).** Log in with YouTube, Instagram or Facebook to show that the account is yours. No account? Verify with Aadhaar instead.
4. **Record a short live video.** It is 10 to 20 seconds, with your phone held upright, and you say what fans can call you about. You cannot upload a video from your gallery. Your phone gives you simple ideas of what to say, in your language. For example: "Hi, I am Neha. I cook home-style food. Call me for easy recipes."
5. **Set your rate and how you take calls.** Choose your price per minute. Then choose **Bookings only** or **Bookings + live** (see below).
6. **Accept the call rules.** Calls are recorded for safety. You agree not to sell on a call (use the Shop instead), not to take payment outside the app, not to share contact details, and to be respectful.
7. **Send it for approval.** A person from our team watches your video and checks your profile. We WhatsApp you when you are approved. If something needs fixing, we tell you what, and you can fix it and send it again.

Until a person approves you, you are not an influencer yet and nobody can call you.

## Your two call options

- **Bookings only.** People can only book a time with you, whether you are online or not.
- **Bookings + live.** When you are online and free, people can call you now. When you are on a call, they can join your queue or book a time. When you are offline, people can still book a time from your profile.

You can change this any time on your dashboard.

## Your intro video in Reels

Your approved intro video plays in Reels, so fans can see and hear you before they call. Reels show only these intro videos. They do not show videos from YouTube or Instagram.

Calls come to your normal phone. We check your ID once, before your first withdrawal.

## What your profile shows

Fans see your display name, your languages, rating, and the topics you have agreed to talk about.

They do **not** see your real name or your phone number. And you never see your fan's number either. Both numbers stay private.
