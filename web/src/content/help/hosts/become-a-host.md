On {{brand.name}}, a host is a real person who talks to callers on a normal phone call and earns money per minute. You can do it from home, whenever you feel like it.

## Who can be a host

- You must be **18 years or older**.
- You must live in India and have your own Indian mobile number.
- You must be able to finish video KYC and Aadhaar verification. KYC just means we check that you are a real person, and that you are who you say you are.

Hosts are not doctors, lawyers, counsellors, CAs or financial advisors. You are there to be a friend, to give company, and to listen.

## What you need

- Your own Indian mobile number with WhatsApp. This is the number your calls will come to.
- Your phone with a camera, for the video KYC.
- Your Aadhaar number and the mobile linked to it, so we can send you an OTP. If the OTP doesn't work, use your DigiLocker sign-in instead (DigiLocker is a Government of India service).

## Steps to start

1. Go to [Become a host](/hosts/join).
2. Sign up with a one-time code (OTP) sent to your WhatsApp. There is no password.
3. Do the **video KYC**. You record a short video so we can see it is really you.
4. Do the **Aadhaar check with an OTP**. We send an OTP to the mobile linked to your Aadhaar. If that doesn't work, you can use DigiLocker instead: sign in and approve sharing your e-Aadhaar. We never store your full Aadhaar number, only the last 4 digits.
5. Pick your **moods**, your **languages**, your **price per minute**, and the **topics** you are happy to talk about.
6. Go **online**. Callers can now find you and ring you.

You do not need to install any app. Calls come to your normal phone.

## What your profile shows

Callers see your display name, your video-KYC badge, your moods, languages, price, rating, and how many people you have talked to. They also see the topics you have agreed to talk about.

They do **not** see your real name, your Aadhaar details or your phone number.

## Before you take your first call

Please read the [host rules](/hosts/rules). They are short and simple. Then read [how earnings work](/help/hosts/host-earnings) and [how to take calls](/help/hosts/taking-calls).

**Remember:** you can go offline any time, and you can decline any call. You are in control.
