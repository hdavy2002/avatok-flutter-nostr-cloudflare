You choose how people can reach you with one switch on your dashboard or in the app. You can change it any time.

| Mode | What people can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Online** | Call you now, join your queue while you are on a call, and book a time |
| **Bookings only** | Book a time. Fans see "Available by appointment". No instant calls, no queue |
| **Offline** | No instant calls, no queue and no new bookings. Calls already confirmed still ring |

When you switch to Offline, we warn you how many booked calls you have. They will still ring.

## On a call

**On a call** shows only while you are really talking. Then people can [join your queue](/help/calling/join-the-queue) or [book a time](/help/calling/book-a-time). After each call you get a short break of about a minute. Then the next person in the queue is connected, and your phone rings first. Your dashboard shows how many people are waiting.

## Pausing the queue

Tap **Pause queue** to stop new people joining and stop calls from the queue. Resume whenever you are ready. If you miss two queued calls in a row, the queue pauses by itself. Your mode does not change.

If you go Offline or to Bookings only, the queue closes and the people waiting are told.

## Booking lengths

People can book **15 minutes, 30 minutes, 1, 2, 3, 4 or 5 hours**. The cost of the first 15 minutes is held when they book. The rest is charged 5 minutes at a time while you talk, and only for the minutes you speak.
