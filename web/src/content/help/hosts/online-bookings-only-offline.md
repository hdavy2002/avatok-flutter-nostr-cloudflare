You choose how people can reach you with one switch on your dashboard.

| Mode | What people can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Online** | Call you now, join your queue while you are on a call, and book a time |
| **Bookings only** | Book a time ("available by appointment"). No instant calls |
| **Offline** | Nothing new. Calls already booked still ring |

## On a call

While you are talking, people see **On a call** and can join your queue or book a time. After each call you get a **60-second break**, then the next person in the queue is called. Your dashboard shows how many people are waiting and their first names.

## Pausing the queue

Tap **Pause queue** to stop new people joining and stop calls from the queue. Resume whenever you are ready. If you miss two queued calls in a row, the queue pauses by itself (your mode does not change).

## Booking lengths

People can book **15 minutes, 30 minutes, 1, 2, 3, 4 or 5 hours**. The cost of the first 15 minutes is held when they book. The rest is charged 5 minutes at a time while you talk, and only for the minutes you speak.
