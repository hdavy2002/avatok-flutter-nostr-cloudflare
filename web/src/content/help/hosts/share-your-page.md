Your public page is where fans see your price and book a call with you. The more people who find it, the more calls you get. Sharing it takes a few seconds.

## On the website

Once your profile is live, open your dashboard. The **Share my page** card is on **Overview** and on your **Profile**. It gives you:

- **Copy link**: paste it anywhere.
- **WhatsApp**, **X** and **Facebook** buttons that open with your link ready to send.
- **Instagram**: Instagram does not let apps post for you, so copy the link and paste it in your bio. The card shows a short tip.
- A **QR code**: show it on a screen or print it, and a fan can scan it with their phone camera.

## In the app

- **Me**: the **Share my page** card has your link, Copy link and Share.
- **Settings**: tap the **Share my page** row.
- **Your profile**: tap the share button.

## Fans can share too

Every influencer's public page has a **Share** button. If a fan likes your page, they can send it to a friend in one tap.

## Good to know

- Your page shows only what you chose to show. Your phone number is never on it. See [Your number stays private](/help/safety-and-privacy/your-number-stays-private).
- Never share your own phone number or UPI ID with a fan. Send them to your page instead.
- Not live yet? Finish your profile first. See [Become an influencer](/help/hosts/become-a-host).
