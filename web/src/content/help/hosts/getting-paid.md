Your money is simple to follow. You earn after each call, and you can send it to your own UPI.

## How you earn

After each **completed call**, your earnings for that call are credited to your account. You can see each call and its amount in your wallet history.

Your fan pays per minute at the rate you have set. To learn more, read [how much you will earn](/help/hosts/host-earnings).

## How to withdraw

1. Sign in and open your wallet.
2. Tap the **Withdraw** button.
3. Use a **UPI ID in your own name**. Withdrawals go **only to UPI**; we do not ask for a bank account.
4. Ask for the amount you want. There is **no minimum withdrawal** and we take **no fee**.

New earnings are held for **7 days** first, then you can withdraw them. You can ask for up to **2 withdrawals a week**. The first time, we do a **one-time ID (KYC) check** and a **UPI check**. After that you will not be asked again. We send the money to your UPI. The UPI must be yours. We do not send money to other people's accounts. This keeps your money safe.

## Watch your request

Each request shows a status: **Waiting for our check**, **Approved**, **Paid** (with the bank reference, called UTR) or **Not paid** (with the reason). You can **cancel** a request while it is waiting.

Test credits can be spent but not withdrawn. Earnings from calls paid with test credits cannot be withdrawn either.

## Never take money outside the platform

Some fans may say, "I will pay you directly, give me your UPI ID." Do not do this.

**Taking money outside the platform is not allowed.** If you do, you can be removed from the platform. You also lose all protection. That means we cannot help you if something goes wrong, because the call was not through us.

Also do not:

- Ask fans for money or gifts.
- Share your UPI ID, phone number, WhatsApp or Instagram DM with a caller.
- Sell anything to a caller, such as products, tips, loans or remedies.

If a caller asks you to move to WhatsApp or to take money directly, say no. You can press # to end the call, block the caller and report it.

## Stay safe with your money

- We will **never** ask for your OTP, UPI PIN, password or bank details. Not on a call, WhatsApp or email.
- If someone says they are from us and asks for these, it is a scam. Do not share. [Report it](/report).

## Need help?

If an earning looks wrong, write to support (@) {{brand.domain}}. Please include the call ID, the date and the time. Never send your OTP, UPI PIN, Aadhaar number or card details.
