Most callers are kind. A few are not. You have simple tools to protect yourself, and you never have to put up with bad behaviour.

## Press # to end, block and report

At any time during a call, press the **# (hash) key**. This does three things at once:

- It ends the call.
- It blocks that caller.
- It reports what happened.

You do not need to say anything first. You never owe an explanation for hanging up.

## The AI also helps

AI follows every live call in real time. It does not look for a list of bad words. It tries to understand what the caller means. It knows the difference between someone describing a bad day and someone being abusive.

If it finds harassment, abuse, threats, sexual pressure or a scam, **the call ends by itself** and the account is flagged. No person listens to your calls, and we do not record them.

## Scam awareness training

We give hosts regular training on spotting spam, scam and fraud calls. Here are some common tricks:

- **OTP asks.** "Please read me the code that came on your phone." Never share it.
- **"KYC update" tricks.** Someone says your account will close unless you update your KYC. Or they send a link to "verify your account".
- **Fake officials.** Someone says he is from your bank, a courier, the police or the government.
- **Sextortion.** Someone is sweet at first, then pushes for private photos or talk, and later threatens you.
- **Sad stories asking for money.** "My mother is in hospital, please send ₹500." Or they push you to buy a product, loan, investment or remedy.

Priya gets a call from a man who says he is from the bank. He slowly asks for her UPI PIN "to fix a problem". She presses #. The call ends, he is blocked, and it is reported.

**Never pay, never send money, and never share OTPs, UPI PIN, card or bank details.** {{brand.name}} will also never ask you for these, on a call, WhatsApp or email.

## After a bad call

If something upset you, you can also [report it](/report). Our trust and safety team reviews it. Scammers are blocked across the whole platform.
