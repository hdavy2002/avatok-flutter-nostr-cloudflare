When a fan orders your T-shirt, you can follow it in **Merch > Orders** in your dashboard or in the app.

## The steps

Every order moves through these steps:

1. **Placed**: the fan has paid.
2. **Approved**: our team checked the order.
3. **Printing**: your T-shirt is being printed.
4. **Shipped**: it is on its way, with the courier name and a tracking link.
5. **Delivered**: it reached the fan.

You do not have to do anything. We print, pack and post it.

## What you can see about the fan

For the fan's privacy you see only their **first name**, their **city and state**, and a **blurred address line**. You never see their phone number, email, full address, UPI, card or any payment details. Please do not try to find them or contact them outside {{brand.name}}.

## What the fan paid

The fan paid one all-in price, **base price + your markup**, labelled **incl. GST & delivery**. The base price is the printing (incl. GST) plus the shipping, shown size by size when you set your price. You earn **60% of your markup** (currently). For example, base ₹444 + markup ₹200 = the fan pays ₹644, and you earn ₹120.

## When you get paid

Your share of the markup is added to your earnings. It can be withdrawn **7 days after the order is delivered** (currently). Use the same **Withdraw** button as for your call earnings. Money goes only to your own UPI ID. See [Getting paid](/help/hosts/getting-paid).

If an order is refunded, for example because it was wrong or damaged, or could not be made, it earns nothing.

## If the printing cost rises

Every paid order is checked before it is printed. If the printing cost has risen a lot since you set your price, the order may be cancelled and the fan refunded in full. You will be asked to raise your price.
