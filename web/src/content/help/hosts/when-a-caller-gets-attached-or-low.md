Most calls are light and friendly. But sometimes a caller is in a very bad place, or starts to depend on you too much. This article tells you what to do. Keep it simple: **be kind, keep boundaries, share help, and flag it.**

## Signs a caller is very low

Listen for lines like these:

- "No one will miss me."
- "I won't be here tomorrow."
- "What is the point of anything?"
- He says goodbye in a strange way, or gives things away.
- He talks about a way or a day to hurt himself.
- He is suddenly calm after being very sad.

## What to do when a caller is very low

1. Stay calm. Speak slowly and kindly.
2. Listen. Do not argue, judge or say "be positive".
3. Do not act like a counsellor or doctor.
4. Do not promise to keep it secret.
5. Say: **"I am not trained to help in a crisis. Please call Tele-MANAS on 14416, or 112 if you are in danger."**
6. End the call kindly so he can get real help.
7. After the call, flag it as a **welfare concern**. It is never a strike for him.

The full words to say, in English and Hindi, are in the [welfare and crisis guide](/hosts/crisis-script).

## Signs a caller is getting too attached

- He calls you every day, often for very long, often late at night.
- He wants only you and gets angry when you are offline.
- He says "I love you" or "you are the only one I have".
- He asks for your number, photo, address or a meeting.
- He spends much more money than before.

## What to do when a caller is too attached

- **Never flirt.** Never say "I love you too".
- **Never promise** "I will always be there for you".
- Never share your number or social media, and never meet.
- Keep the talk friendly and general.
- **Flag the caller early.** Do not wait until it gets bad.

You do not have to reject him yourself. When you flag a caller, **the platform limits or ends the contact**. He only sees "This host isn't available right now." Your name is never linked to a refusal.

## Never do these

- Give medical, medicine, legal, money or relationship advice.
- Keep a sad or attached caller on the line to earn more minutes.
- Encourage him to depend on you.

Doing these can lead to removal. Your safety and his safety both matter more than minutes.

## Example

Ravi calls Neha every night for two hours. One night he says, "If you leave me, I will do something to myself." Neha stays calm. She says she cares, that she is not trained for a crisis, and gives him Tele-MANAS 14416 and 112. She ends the call gently and flags it as a welfare concern. The platform takes over from there. Neha did everything right.
