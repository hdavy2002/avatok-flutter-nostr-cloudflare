Taking calls is easy. You go online, your phone rings, and you choose to accept or decline. You are always in control.

## Going online and offline

You choose **Online**, **Bookings only** or **Offline** any time from your dashboard or the app. When you are Online, your fans can call you now. Bookings only means "available by appointment". Offline means no new calls, queue or bookings. Read [which mode to choose](/help/hosts/online-bookings-only-offline).

If a fan has hearted you as a favourite, they can tap "Notify me". They get one WhatsApp when you are back. You can see how many people have hearted you and asked to be notified.

While you are talking, fans see **On a call** and can join your queue or book a time. After your call you get a short break of about a minute, then your phone rings for the next person.

## What happens when a call comes

1. A fan picks you and taps call.
2. **Our system calls you first.** Nobody is connected to you yet.
3. You hear a short **private announcement** that a fan wants to talk. It tells you the fan's display handle and how many times they have called before.
4. Press **1** to accept. Press **2** to decline.
5. If you accept, we call the fan and join the two calls together.
6. Both of you hear a short safety notice.

Both calls come from our platform number. Your private number is never shared with the fan, and theirs is never shared with you.

## The 10-second rule

If you do not press anything within **10 seconds**, it counts as a decline. Nothing is charged. Nothing bad happens to your profile.

## Declining is fine

You can decline any call. You do not owe anyone a reason. If you feel tired, busy or unsure, press 2. You can also hang up at any time during a call, or press **#** to end the call and block the person.

## Set your topics

On your profile, you choose which topics you are happy to talk about. This is called topic consent. Fans can see these topics before they call.

Only talk about topics you have agreed to. If a fan pushes you to talk about something you did not agree to, you can say no, or end the call.

## Keep it simple and kind

Many fans just want to say hello and hear from someone they admire. Be warm and friendly. You are not a doctor or a counsellor, so do not give medical, legal or money advice.
