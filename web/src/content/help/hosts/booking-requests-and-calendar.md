Fans can book you for 15 minutes up to 5 hours. Here is how to manage it.

## Set your availability

Open **Availability** in the app or on the website. Add your weekly hours and any special days. It is **one schedule**: whatever you set in the app or on the website is the same everywhere.

Days that are already booked are **crossed out** in Availability and in your Calendar, so you can see your time at a glance.

## Your overview

Your Overview shows how many **days are booked** and how many **requests are waiting** for you.

## Accept or decline

If you accept requests first, a new booking arrives as a **request**.

1. Open the request in the app or on the website.
2. Tap **Accept** or **Decline**.
3. A declined or expired request is refunded in full to the fan's wallet. Nothing is charged to anyone.

A request not accepted within **24 hours** (or one hour before the call, if that is sooner) is cancelled by itself. You get a nudge a few hours before.

## When the call time comes

Confirmed bookings ring at their time, even if you are Offline. We WhatsApp both of you about 1 hour and 10 minutes before. See [Online, Bookings only or Offline](/help/hosts/online-bookings-only-offline).

The fan's first 15 minutes are held when she books. The rest is charged 5 minutes at a time while you talk. You earn for the minutes you speak. See [how much you will earn](/help/hosts/host-earnings).
