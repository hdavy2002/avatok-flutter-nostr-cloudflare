Your fans pay per minute, at the rate you set. You earn your share of each connected minute. Here is how it works, in plain words.

## The simple rule

The caller is charged per minute at **your rate**. {{brand.name}} works this out for the exact seconds you are connected. Your earnings for each call are shown in rupees, with the payout status. You can see your current rate and your share in your dashboard.

## A simple example

Imagine your rate is a round number, ₹10 a minute, only to make the sum easy. Your fan Rohit from Pune calls you and you talk for 15 minutes. He is charged for 15 minutes at your rate. Your earnings for the call are your share of that amount. Your dashboard shows the exact figure after the call.

If the call never connects, nobody pays anything and nothing is earned.

## Things to know

- **Calls are not guaranteed.** Some days are busy and some days are quiet.
- Earning starts only when both of you are connected.
- A fan needs money in their wallet before a call can start.

## Grow your fans

Fans who enjoy talking to you will call again. Be kind, be on time, and tell your followers on your own channels that you take calls here. Never share your phone number, UPI ID or social DMs on a call.

To see how to take your money out, read [Getting paid](/help/hosts/getting-paid). To see the full rates page, go to [influencer rates](/hosts/rates).
