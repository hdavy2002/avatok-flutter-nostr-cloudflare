Your fans pay per minute, at the rate you set. A new influencer starts at ₹5 a minute, and ₹5 is the lowest rate. You can raise it any time. You earn your share of each connected minute. Here is how it works, in plain words.

## The simple rule

The caller is charged per minute of the phone call at **your rate**, only for the minutes spoken. **We keep ₹2 a minute plus 40% of the rate above ₹2. You keep the rest.** The part we keep covers the phone call, platform fees and GST. Your earnings for each call are shown in rupees in your dashboard, with the withdrawal status.

## A simple example

Say your rate is ₹20 a minute. Your fan Rohit from Pune calls you and you talk for 15 minutes. He is charged ₹300 (15 × ₹20). For each minute we keep ₹2, plus 40% of the other ₹18, which is ₹7.20. So we keep ₹9.20 and you earn **₹10.80** a minute. For 15 minutes you earn **₹162**, before TDS or your own taxes.

If the influencer declines, cancels or does not answer, nobody pays anything and nothing is earned.

## Things to know

- **Calls are not guaranteed.** Some days are busy and some days are quiet.
- Earning starts only once we have rung both of you and joined the call.
- A fan needs at least ₹20, or the cost of 5 minutes at your rate if that is more, before a call can start.
- Earnings are held for 7 days, then you can withdraw them with the Withdraw button.

## Grow your fans

Fans who enjoy talking to you will call again. Be kind, be on time, and tell your followers on your own channels that you take calls here. Never share your phone number, UPI ID or social DMs on a call.
