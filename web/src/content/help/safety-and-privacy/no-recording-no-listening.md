Your talk is yours. {{brand.name}} does not record your calls. Nobody listens in.

## What we do not do

- **We do not record calls.**
- **We do not store call audio.**
- **No person monitors or listens to live calls.**
- We do not keep a transcript.

Please do not record the other person yourself either. It is against our rules. See [Community Guidelines](/community-guidelines).

## What about the AI?

The AI follows a live call only to spot danger, like abuse or a scam. It ends the call if needed. It does not save the audio. Read how it works in [How does AI keep my calls safe?](/help/safety-and-privacy/how-ai-keeps-calls-safe).

## What small records we keep

We keep only a few facts about each call:

- who called whom (as account IDs)
- the time
- how long it lasted
- the price
- whether a person or a safety action ended the call

That is all. No audio and no transcript.

## Why we keep these records

- **Billing.** You see every call charge in your wallet history. If you are charged for a call that never connected, we need these facts to put the money back.
- **Complaints.** If someone reports a problem, we can check when the call happened and how it ended.

## We never sell your data

**We never sell personal data.** Your legal name, Aadhaar details and phone number are never shown on a profile or shared with the other person.

## An example

Meera talks to a host for 20 minutes about a rough day. Later, she asks, "Can someone hear what I said?" The answer is no. Nothing she said was saved. We only kept the time, the length, the price and who ended the call.
