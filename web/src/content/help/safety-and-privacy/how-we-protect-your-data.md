{{brand.name}} follows India's data protection law: the **Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023** and the **DPDP Rules, 2025**. This page explains, in simple words, what we keep and what you can do.

## Your number and photos are protected

- **Your number is masked.** The other person never sees your phone number. Both calls come from our platform number.
- **Profile pictures can be AI-made avatars.** Influencers may not show their real photo on their profile. An influencer can also record a short voice introduction in their own voice. Our team listens to it before it goes live, and it is labelled "Recorded by the influencer". The person on the call is a real, verified influencer.

## What we collect from callers

To call an influencer, we keep:

- your **WhatsApp number** (checked with a one-time code)
- the **number you receive calls on** (it can be the same number)
- your **wallet top-ups** by UPI
- your **call details**: the time, how long, the price and how the call ended

No human monitors your call. Influencers never see your number. They see only your caller name.

*Example: Rohan tops up Rs 100 and talks for 10 minutes. We keep the top-up, the time, the length and the price. We do not keep what he said.*

## What we collect from influencers

Influencers go through more checks, because they earn money and must be real adults. In short: a WhatsApp code, your Aadhaar with an OTP (or DigiLocker if OTP doesn't work), a 10-second selfie video, and a check of your UPI and bank account. We never store your full Aadhaar number. We keep **only the last 4 digits**. Read all the details on [how we verify influencers](/hosts/kyc).

## How we keep your data safe

- We **never sell** your data. We never use it for advertising.
- It is stored **encrypted**, in private storage.
- Only a small, authorised team can open it, and **every access is written in a log**.
- We share it only with the partners who help us run the service (for example the one that checks Aadhaar, and the one that pays influencers), or with a government authority when the law requires.

## How long we keep it

- **Influencers:** while you are an influencer, and for **1 year after** you leave. Then we delete it.
- **Records the law asks us to keep:** the law says some records and logs must be kept for at least 1 year. We keep those as long as the law needs, and no longer than necessary.

## Your rights

You can ask us to:

- **See** the data we hold about you
- **Correct** anything that is wrong
- **Delete** your account and data (except records the law says we must keep)
- **Withdraw your consent** at any time
- **Nominate** someone to use your rights for you if you cannot
- **Complain** if you are unhappy

We reply as fast as we can, and always within the time the law allows. That is **90 days at most**. If you are still not happy, you can go to the **Data Protection Board of India**.

*Example: Priya is a caller and wants to leave. She taps Delete my account. We delete her data, but we keep the billing records the law needs for a while.*

## If something goes wrong

If a data breach ever affects you, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board of India, as the law requires.

## How to reach us

Write to our Grievance Officer on the [grievance page](/grievance). To close your account, read [Delete your account](/help/account-and-help/delete-your-account).
