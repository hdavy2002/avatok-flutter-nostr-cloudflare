<!-- rebrand: reviewed -->

Your account is paused while a person on our team reviews it.

## Why it happens

- You have **three active strikes** (a strike is active for 90 days), or
- we think someone **may be in danger**, so we pause the account at once.

## If you are a fan

- You cannot call or book while the account is paused.
- You can still **sign in** and **withdraw your wallet money** with the Withdraw button.

## If you are an influencer

- You are **hidden** from Reels and search.
- Calls, the queue and new bookings are **off**.
- Booked calls are **cancelled**, and the fans get a **full refund** to their wallets.
- Your **earnings are held** until the review ends.

## What happens next

- We WhatsApp you when the review is decided.
- You can [appeal a strike](/help/safety-and-privacy/how-to-appeal-a-strike). If it is accepted, the strike is removed and your pause can end.
- **Only a person can ban an account.** A ban is permanent, and the phone number cannot be used to sign up again.
- If a ban is confirmed, fans of the flagged calls are refunded first from the held money. The rest is paid to the influencer's UPI ID, as for a closed account.

Example: Asha has three active strikes. Her account is paused and her booked calls are cancelled. The fans get their money back. Our team reviews it in a few days.
