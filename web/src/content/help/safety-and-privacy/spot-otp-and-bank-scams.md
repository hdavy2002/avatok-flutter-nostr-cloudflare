Some people try to cheat others on calls. They want your OTP, your UPI PIN or your bank details. This article shows you how to spot them.

## The golden rule

**{{brand.name}} will NEVER ask for your OTP, UPI PIN, password or bank details. Not on a call, not on WhatsApp, not on email.**

Nobody real needs your OTP or PIN. If anyone asks, it is a scam.

## Lines scammers use

Watch out for lines like these:

- "Sir, your KYC will expire today. Tell me the OTP."
- "Madam, I am calling from your bank. Your card is blocked."
- "Please click this link to verify your account."
- "I am from the courier. Tell me the code to get your parcel."
- "This is the police. Pay now or you will be in trouble."
- "Invest ₹5,000 today and get double money."
- "Take this loan, no papers needed."
- "Buy this remedy and your problems will go away."

They may also say your Aadhaar number or card number is needed. It is not.

## Slow build-up

Not every scammer starts with a scam. Some are friendly for 10 minutes. Then they ask for "just one OTP". Our AI understands this slow build-up. It follows the intention, not only the words.

## What our AI does

- It ends the call.
- The account is flagged.
- Spammers and scammers are blocked across the whole platform. They cannot call any other host.
- If they try again from a new account, we match and block them.

Hosts also get regular training to spot these tricks.

## What you should do

1. Do not share anything. Say "no" and hang up.
2. Press # to end the call, block the person and report.
3. Never share OTP, UPI PIN, Aadhaar number or card details with anyone.
4. Tell us at [report form](/report).
