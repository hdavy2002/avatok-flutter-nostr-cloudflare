## LGBTQ+ friendly influencers

- Influencers can show **LGBTQ+ friendly** on their profile.
- It is **their own choice**, off by default, and they can change it at any time.
- We never guess anything about anyone.

## Verified customers only

Influencers on the **monthly influencer plan** can take calls only from customers who have completed full KYC. Verification confirms who the customer is. It does not check orientation or gender identity, and it does not guarantee behaviour.

*Example: Arjun is an LGBTQ+ friendly influencer. He turns on Verified customers only, so every caller's identity is known before they can ring him.*

## You never have to tell us

Customers are never asked about their orientation to make a call. Share only what you are comfortable saying aloud.

## Friendly conversation only

The rules are the same as everywhere else. It is for friendly talking. No flirting, no sexual talk, no asking for photos, and no meeting up. Read the [Community Guidelines](/community-guidelines).

## Stay safe: simple tips

- **Never share** your phone number, your photos or your home address.
- **Never pay outside the platform.** Not by UPI, not by gift card, not by any other way.
- **Press # at any time.** The call ends and the person is blocked.
- **Threats or demands for money: report right away.** Some bad people try to scare you, for example by saying they will tell your family or friends unless you pay. This is extortion. Do not pay. End the call, press #, and [report them](/report) at once. If you feel in danger, call **112**.

You will not get in trouble for being the one who was threatened. We will help.
