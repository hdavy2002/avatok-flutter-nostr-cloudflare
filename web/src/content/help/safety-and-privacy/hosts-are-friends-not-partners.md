It is normal to like talking to someone who listens well. Many callers find a host they enjoy and call again. That is fine. But a host is a friendly voice, **not a partner**. This article explains why.

## Hosts are friends, not partners

Hosts are real, independent people. They offer friendly conversation for a per-minute price. {{brand.name}} is **not a dating or relationship service**.

A host being warm and kind to you is them doing their work well. It is not a promise of love or a future together.

## What hosts will not do

- Flirt or say "I love you".
- Share their phone number or social media.
- Meet you in person.
- Promise to always be there.

If you ask for these things again and again, it can lead to a strike. See [strikes and bans](/help/safety-and-privacy/strikes-and-bans).

## Healthy limits

When calling launches, the platform will have limits to keep things healthy for everyone:

- A **daily time limit** with the same host.
- A short **cooling-off gap** before calling the same host again.
- A check before large top-ups.

## "This host isn't available right now"

If you see this, the host may be offline or busy, or a limit may apply. **It is not a rejection.** The host is not angry with you. It is a platform rule, the same for everyone. You can talk to someone else, or come back later.

## Example

Aman calls Pooja every night. He starts to feel she is the only person who understands him. One day he sees "not available right now". He feels hurt. But it is only a daily limit. Aman calls a friend from college instead, and talks to Pooja again another day. That is a healthy balance.

## If you feel very low

If you feel hopeless or think about hurting yourself, please call **Tele-MANAS 14416** (free, 24×7) or **112**. Hosts are not counsellors. Read [use it in a healthy way](/wellbeing) and [need urgent help](/help/account-and-help/need-urgent-help).
