It is normal to admire someone whose videos or posts you love. Many fans call their favourite influencer more than once. That is fine. But an influencer is a person you admire, **not a partner**. This article explains why.

## Influencers are people you admire, not partners

Influencers are real, independent people. They take calls from their fans for a per-minute price. {{brand.name}} is **not a dating or relationship service**.

An influencer being warm and kind to you is them being friendly and polite. It is not a promise of love, friendship or a future together.

## What influencers will not do

- Flirt or say "I love you".
- Share their phone number or social media.
- Meet you in person.
- Take money or gifts outside the platform.
- Promise to always be there.

If you ask for these things again and again, it can lead to a strike after our team reviews a report. See [strikes and bans](/help/safety-and-privacy/strikes-and-bans).

## For influencers too

The same rules protect you. Never flirt, never share your number or social handles in a call, never ask a fan for money or gifts, and never meet a fan. Keep it friendly and keep your boundaries. If a fan is getting too attached, read [what to do](/help/hosts/when-a-caller-gets-attached-or-low).

## Healthy limits

When calling launches, the platform will have limits to keep things healthy for everyone:

- A **daily time limit** with the same influencer.
- A short **cooling-off gap** before calling the same influencer again.
- A check before large top-ups.

## "This influencer isn't available right now"

If you see this, the influencer may be offline or busy, or a limit may apply. **It is not a rejection.** The influencer is not angry with you. It is a platform rule, the same for everyone. You can come back later.

## Example

Aman is a big fan of a travel vlogger and calls every night. He starts to feel the vlogger is the only person who understands him. One day he sees "not available right now". He feels hurt. But it is only a daily limit. Aman calls a friend from college instead, and talks to the vlogger again another day. That is a healthy balance.

## If you feel very low

If you feel hopeless or think about hurting yourself, please call **Tele-MANAS 14416** (free, 24×7) or **112**. Influencers are not counsellors. Read [use it in a healthy way](/wellbeing) and [need urgent help](/help/account-and-help/need-urgent-help).
