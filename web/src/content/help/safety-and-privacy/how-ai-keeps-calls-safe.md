On {{brand.name}}, you talk to your favourite influencer on a private phone call. Here is how we keep that safe. It is a short, honest list.

## Calls are recorded. Nobody listens live.

**Every call is recorded for safety, and nobody listens to it live.** After the call, an AI turns the recording into text and checks it for rule breaks. Read more in [Are calls recorded?](/help/safety-and-privacy/are-calls-recorded).

## What keeps you safe

- **Masked numbers.** Both calls come from a {{brand.name}} number. Your phone number and the influencer's phone number are never shared with each other.
- **Press # any time.** It ends the call, blocks the other person and reports what happened. You do not need to explain.
- **A check after every call.** If the AI is very sure a rule was broken, a strike can be given straight away. If it is not sure, our safety team looks first.
- **Report and block.** You can [report](/report) anyone who breaks a rule. The call is then checked.
- **Strikes you can appeal.** Read [What is a strike?](/help/safety-and-privacy/what-is-a-strike) and [How do I appeal a strike?](/help/safety-and-privacy/how-to-appeal-a-strike).
- **Only a person bans.** Nobody is banned by a machine.
- **Scam warnings.** We tell you the common tricks. See [spot OTP and bank scams](/help/safety-and-privacy/spot-otp-and-bank-scams).
- **We never ask for secrets.** {{brand.name}} never asks for your OTP, UPI PIN or passwords. If anyone does, it is a scam.

## What to do if a call feels wrong

1. Press **#**. The call ends and the person is blocked.
2. [Report](/report) what happened, so our team can look at it.
3. Do not send money, share an OTP or move to WhatsApp, whatever the reason. Never pay anyone outside {{brand.name}}.

Example: Anita is on a call with a person who starts asking for her UPI PIN "for a small check". She presses #. The call ends, he is blocked, and she reports it. The call is checked and he gets a strike.

## If we get something wrong

If you think a strike was a mistake, you can [appeal it once](/help/safety-and-privacy/how-to-appeal-a-strike). A person will review it.

If you talk about self-harm, that is never a strike. We only do a welfare follow-up, and the number to call is **Tele-MANAS 14416**.
