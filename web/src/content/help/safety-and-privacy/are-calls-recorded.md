**Yes. Every call on {{brand.name}} is recorded for safety. Nobody listens to a call live.**

## Why we record

We record calls to stop selling, payments outside {{brand.name}}, contact-sharing and abuse. Women-only calls are recorded and checked in the same way.

## What happens after a call

1. The recording goes to private storage. It is never on a public page.
2. An AI turns it into text and checks it against the [community guidelines](/community-guidelines).
3. If nothing is wrong, the recording is deleted right away.
4. If the AI is very sure a rule was broken, a strike can be given straight away. If it is not sure, our safety team looks first. See [What is a strike?](/help/safety-and-privacy/what-is-a-strike).

## Who can read it

Only named members of our safety team. They first see only the flagged line and two lines before and after it. To open the whole text they must type a reason, and every opening is logged. A strike message shows you only your own words, never the other person's.

## How long we keep things

- **Recording, nothing found:** deleted right after it is turned into text.
- **Recording, flagged or reported:** 30 days.
- **Text of the call:** 30 days if nothing was found, 1 year if flagged.
- **Check results, strikes and decisions:** 1 year.

## What you should do

Do not share your phone number, UPI ID or address on a call. Never pay anyone outside {{brand.name}}. If someone asks, say no and [report it](/report) after the call.

Example: Ravi tells a fan, "Send the money to my UPI and I will tell you more." The call is recorded. The AI finds the payment request, and Ravi gets a strike.
