If you think a strike is wrong, you can appeal it. **You can appeal each strike once.**

## How to appeal

Choose one:

1. **WhatsApp.** Reply **APPEAL** to the message we sent about the strike.
2. **App or website.** Open your safety page and tap **Appeal** next to the strike. Add a short note if you want.

## What happens next

1. We send you a WhatsApp message to say your appeal was received.
2. A person on our safety team reviews it. They look at the flagged part of the call.
3. We tell you the decision.
4. If your appeal is accepted, **the strike is removed**. If not, it stays until it expires after 90 days.

## Good to know

- You see only your own words in the strike message, never the other person's.
- A paused account can still appeal and can still withdraw wallet money.
- If you still disagree, use the [grievance page](/grievance).

Example: Kabir is told he asked for a phone number. He was actually reading out the helpline number. He replies APPEAL, a person listens to the flagged part, and the strike is removed.
