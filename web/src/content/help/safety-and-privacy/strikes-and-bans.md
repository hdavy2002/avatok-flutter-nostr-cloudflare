{{brand.name}} has simple rules to keep everyone safe. If someone breaks them, they get a strike. This article explains how strikes and bans work.

## How a strike happens

1. A person reports a call.
2. Our team reviews the report.
3. Only after that review is a strike confirmed.

**A report is not a strike. A strike is confirmed only after our team reviews it.**

## Three strikes

**Three confirmed strikes mean a permanent ban.**

Here are some things that can lead to a strike:

- abuse, threats or gaali
- asking for or sharing phone numbers, WhatsApp, Instagram or UPI IDs
- arranging to meet
- selling anything
- asking for money outside the platform

See the full list in [Community Guidelines](/community-guidelines).

## Immediate bans

Some things are too serious to wait for three strikes. These can lead to an **immediate ban**:

- threats
- sexual harassment
- fraud or scams

## What a ban means

A banned account:

- cannot make or take calls
- cannot register again with the same phone number

## Wallet money is safe

**A banned user can still sign in and withdraw wallet money with the Withdraw button.** We never take your money as a penalty.

## What if there was a mistake?

We can make mistakes. If you think a strike or ban is wrong, go to [grievance page](/grievance). A person will review it.

Mentioning self-harm is never a strike. We only do a welfare follow-up. If you need help right now, see [emergency help](/emergency).

## An example

Sameer keeps asking an influencer for her WhatsApp number. She says no. He still asks again. She presses # and reports him. Our team reviews it and confirms a strike. If this happens two more times, his account will be banned for good.
