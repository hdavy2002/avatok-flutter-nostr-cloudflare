{{brand.name}} has simple rules to keep everyone safe. If someone breaks them on a call, they can get a strike. This article is the short version. Details are in [What is a strike?](/help/safety-and-privacy/what-is-a-strike).

## How a strike happens

1. Every call is recorded for safety. Nobody listens live.
2. After the call, an AI checks it. A report from a fan or an influencer also sends the call for a check.
3. If the AI is very sure a rule was broken, a strike can be given straight away. If it is not sure, our safety team looks first.

**A strike is for one call and one person, a fan or an influencer.**

## What can lead to a strike

- abuse, threats or gaali
- sexual talk or adult services
- selling anything (influencers can use the Shop instead)
- financial tips, loans or network marketing
- asking for or taking payment outside {{brand.name}}
- sharing or asking for phone numbers, UPI IDs or addresses

See the full list in [Community Guidelines](/community-guidelines).

## Three strikes pause the account

A strike counts for **90 days**, then it expires. **Three active strikes pause the account.** Read [Why is my account paused?](/help/safety-and-privacy/why-is-my-account-paused).

## Only a person can ban

A ban is permanent, and the phone number cannot be used to sign up again. Only a person on our team can ban an account. Threats, sexual harassment or fraud can lead to an immediate ban.

## Wallet money is safe

**A paused or banned user can still sign in and withdraw wallet money with the Withdraw button.** We never take your money as a penalty.

## What if there was a mistake?

You can appeal each strike once. Read [How do I appeal a strike?](/help/safety-and-privacy/how-to-appeal-a-strike). If you still disagree, use the [grievance page](/grievance).

Mentioning self-harm is never a strike. We only do a welfare follow-up. If you need help right now, see [emergency help](/emergency).
