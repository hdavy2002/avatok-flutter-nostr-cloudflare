You can close your {{brand.name}} account if you want to leave. We settle your money first, then delete the account.

## Step 1: Settle your money

1. Sign in and open **Close my account**.
2. Add your **UPI ID** and do the one-time **ID check**, if you have not already. You can do both on the website.
3. The page shows the exact amount you will receive.

Here is what is paid:

- Your **wallet balance**, as the Wallet page shows it. That is the amount **after GST**, and **without test credits**.
- It is paid to **your own UPI ID**, in **whole rupees**. The part below ₹1 is not paid. It is removed with the account.
- If you are an influencer with earnings still in the 7-day hold, your money **waits until the release date**. The Close page and a WhatsApp tell you the amount and the date.

Money is never sent back to your card. If you would rather take it out yourself first, read [withdraw your wallet money](/help/wallet-and-payments/withdraw-your-balance).

## Step 2: Delete after 30 days

Once your money is settled, your account is deleted after a **30-day wait**. You can sign in with a one-time code (OTP). There is no password. The [data deletion page](/data-deletion) has the full steps.

## Need only a break?

You do not have to close. Tap **Pause notifications** to pause only the Notify-me WhatsApps. You can resume them any time. Influencers can also go Offline.

## What we keep

We keep only simple call details:

- Who called whom (as account IDs)
- The time and the duration
- The price
- How the call ended

We keep these for billing and for checking complaints. They do not include anything you said on the call.

We never sell personal data. To know more, read our [privacy page](/privacy).

## Good to know

- Your real name and phone number were never shown on your profile or shared with other people.
- A banned account cannot re-register with the same phone number.

## Need help?

If you are stuck, write to support (@) {{brand.domain}}. Please never send your OTP, UPI PIN, Aadhaar number or card details.

If you feel something is unfair, you can use the [grievance page](/grievance).
