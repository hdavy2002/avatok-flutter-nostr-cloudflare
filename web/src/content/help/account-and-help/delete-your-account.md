You can delete your {{brand.name}} account if you want to leave. Please do one important thing first: take out your money.

## Step 1: Withdraw your wallet money

Anyone can withdraw wallet money with the Withdraw button. We send it to your UPI after a one-time ID (KYC) and UPI check. Test credits cannot be withdrawn.

1. Sign in and open your wallet.
2. Tap **Withdraw** and take out what you want to keep.
3. Then close your account from the close-account page. Your account is deleted once everything is settled.

Doing this first keeps your money safe. Read [withdraw your wallet money](/help/wallet-and-payments/withdraw-your-balance) for the full steps.

If you are an influencer, your latest earnings are held for 7 days. See [getting paid](/help/hosts/getting-paid).

## Step 2: Follow the delete steps

Go to the [data deletion page](/data-deletion). It has the steps to ask for your account to be deleted. Follow them one by one.

You can sign in with a one-time code (OTP). There is no password to remember.

## What we keep

We keep only simple call details:

- Who called whom (as account IDs)
- The time and the duration
- The price
- How the call ended

We keep these for billing and for checking complaints. They do not include anything you said on the call.

We never sell personal data. To know more, read our [privacy page](/privacy).

## Good to know

- Your real name and phone number were never shown on your profile or shared with other people.
- A banned account cannot re-register with the same phone number.
- If you only want a break, you do not have to delete. You can just stop calling, or go offline if you are an influencer.

## Need help?

If you are stuck, write to support (@) {{brand.domain}}. Please never send your OTP, UPI PIN, Aadhaar number or card details.

If you feel something is unfair, you can use the [grievance page](/grievance).
