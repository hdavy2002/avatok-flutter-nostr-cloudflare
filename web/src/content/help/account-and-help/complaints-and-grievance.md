If something went wrong and you are not happy, tell us. We want to hear it, and a real person will look at it.

## When to use the grievance page

Use the [grievance page](/grievance) if:

- You think a charge is wrong, and you need more help. Start with [billing errors](/help/wallet-and-payments/billing-errors).
- Our AI ended your call or flagged your account, and you think it was a **mistake**.
- You were given a strike or a ban, and you think it was **unfair**.
- You had a problem that the help pages did not solve.

## AI mistakes and ban reviews

AI follows live calls to keep everyone safe. It tries to understand what a person means, not just the words. But no system is perfect.

If you believe the AI got it wrong, raise it on the grievance page. **A person reviews it.** AI flags and reports are also reviewed by our trust and safety team before a strike is confirmed.

Three confirmed strikes lead to a permanent ban. Threats, sexual harassment or fraud can lead to an immediate ban. If you think a strike or ban is wrong, ask for a review.

Remember, a banned account can still sign in, and unused purchased tokens can be refunded on request through Google Play within 180 days. Your money is never taken as a penalty.

## What to include

Please give us clear details, so we can check fast:

1. Your **name or display name** and the account you used.
2. The **call ID**, the **date** and the **time**.
3. The **amount**, if it is about money.
4. A short note on **what happened** and what you want us to do.

Rohit was cut off in a call when he was only talking about a bad day at work. He goes to the grievance page, writes the call ID and time, and says the AI made a mistake. A person looks at it.

## What not to send

**Never send your OTP, UPI PIN, password, Aadhaar number or card details.** We will never ask for them. Not on a call, WhatsApp or email.

## Other ways to reach us

You can also write to support (@) {{brand.domain}}. Please add the call ID, the date and the time.

If it is a rule break by another person, use the [report page](/report). If you need help right now, read [need urgent help](/help/account-and-help/need-urgent-help).
