Your profile is how {{brand.name}} knows you. You can edit it any time from your dashboard.

## What you can add

- **First name and last name.** These stay private.
- **The name influencers see.** This is the name shown to others. Pick one you are happy to share.
- **City.**
- **Languages** you speak.
- **What you like to talk about.**

Your phone number is never shown to the other person. See [Your number stays private](/help/safety-and-privacy/your-number-stays-private).

## Your handle

Every account has a **handle**. If you never chose one, we make one for you. A handle **cannot be changed**.

## Keeping up with updates

When our Terms, Privacy or Community guidelines change, you are asked **once** to accept the update after you sign in.
