If someone breaks the rules, tell us. Reporting helps keep {{brand.name}} safe for everyone.

## When to report

Please report if someone:

- Is abusive, uses gaali or makes threats.
- Pushes you for sexual talk or sexual pictures.
- Asks for your OTP, UPI PIN, card or bank details.
- Pretends to be from a bank, courier, police or government.
- Asks for money outside the platform.
- Asks for your phone number, WhatsApp, Instagram or UPI ID.
- Tries to sell you something, such as products, loans or remedies.
- Wants to meet you, or says they are under 18.

A call that was just boring or not your type is not a rule break. You can rate the host and try someone else.

## How to report

1. Go to the [report page](/report).
2. Add the **call ID**. You can find it in your call history.
3. Add the **profile name** of the person.
4. Write **what happened**, in a few short lines.

If you are on a call and feel unsafe, you do not have to wait. Press **#** on your phone. It ends the call, blocks that person and reports the incident at once.

## What happens next

- Our **trust and safety team reviews** your report.
- AI flags and reports are checked by a person before a strike is confirmed.
- **Three confirmed strikes** lead to a permanent ban.
- Threats, sexual harassment or fraud can lead to an immediate ban.
- Scammers are blocked across the whole platform.

We keep the call details that help us check. These are who called whom, the time, the duration, and whether a person or a safety action ended the call. We do not record calls, so there is no audio to listen to.

## What we cannot do

We are **not an emergency service**. If you are in danger, call **112**. If you are in a bad place in your mind, read [need urgent help](/help/account-and-help/need-urgent-help).

## You can also block

You can block someone too. See [block someone](/help/account-and-help/block-someone).

If you think we made a mistake, use the [grievance page](/grievance).
