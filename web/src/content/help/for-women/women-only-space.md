The women-only space is a separate lane on {{brand.name}}. Only women talk to women here. It is for women who want to speak with someone who understands.

## What it is

It is a private lane inside the platform. The caller is a woman. The host is a woman. No man can be on either side.

It works like the rest of the platform: you pick a host, you see the price, you pay per minute. Your number stays masked. The difference is who can be there.

## Who can enter

**Both the caller and the host must complete two checks** before the lane even appears:

1. **Video KYC.** KYC means "know your customer". You do a short live video so we can see you are a real person.
2. **Aadhaar KYC.** You verify with your Aadhaar by OTP. This shows that you are a woman.

Your Aadhaar details are never shown on your profile or shared with the other person.

## It is hidden from others

**Accounts that are not verified cannot see the lane or call into it.** It does not show up for them at all. A man cannot just click a button and enter.

## Why a man cannot fake it

- He needs to pass a live video check.
- He needs Aadhaar verification that shows a woman.
- This is checked on both sides, caller and host.
- Until both are done, the lane does not appear.

So there is no easy way to get in by pretending.

## An example

Neha is a caller from Jaipur. She wants to talk about something personal. She finishes her video KYC and Aadhaar KYC. Now she sees the women-only lane. She picks a host who is also a verified woman and starts her call. Nobody else can see this lane.

## Rules still apply

The rest of the safety system works here too. The AI follows calls, # ends and blocks, and calls are not recorded. Harassment and sexual pressure are still not allowed.
