Talking about your body can feel hard. On {{brand.name}}, there is a safe place for it. This article explains what is fine, and where the limits are.

## In the women-only space

In the [women-only space](/help/for-women/women-only-space), health talk is normal. You can talk about:

- periods
- PCOS
- pregnancy
- menopause
- intimate health

Nobody will look at you strangely. The AI is tuned to understand this kind of talk. **If you describe a symptom or a bad experience, you will not be flagged.** The same is true for the host.

## What is still not allowed

Even here, these are not allowed:

- harassment
- sexual pressure
- explicit or erotic talk

**Explicit or erotic talk is never allowed, in any lane, with any host.**

## Outside the women-only space

Sexual or suggestive talk is not allowed on the platform. There is one exception: a real health conversation with a host who has **agreed to that topic**.

Every host profile shows the topics the host has agreed to talk about. This is called topic consent. It is how you know a host is comfortable.

- Check the host's profile first.
- Talk about a health topic only if it is listed there.
- If it is not listed, do not bring it up.

## An example

Divya has had painful periods for months. She is shy. She goes to the women-only lane and picks a host. She says, "My periods are very painful and irregular. I am scared." The AI understands this is a health worry, so the call continues. The host listens kindly.

Later, a different caller tries the same topic with a host who did not list it. The host can say no or end the call. That is the host's right.

## This is not medical advice

Hosts are friendly people to talk to. They are not doctors. They do not give medical advice or tell you what medicine to take.

**For any health problem, please see a doctor.**

If you feel very low or unsafe, you can call **Tele-MANAS 14416** (free, 24x7). For danger, call **112**. See [emergency help](/emergency).
