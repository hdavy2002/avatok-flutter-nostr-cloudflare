Is this for creators or fans?

Both. Creators offer paid live streams and private 1:1 video sessions. Fans browse the marketplace and book an experience that interests them.

Do I have to leave my social channels?

No. Keep creating on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram or anywhere else. Share your Saathum listing with your audience as another way to connect.

How much can I earn?

Your earnings depend on your prices, bookings, fees and the sessions you deliver. Earnings are not guaranteed. Review current pricing and fees.

How do local payouts work?

Available currencies and payment methods depend on your country, verification and eligibility. See payout information before creating your offer.

What should I check before booking?

Read the listing’s session format, schedule, inclusions and cancellation terms. Keep communication within the appropriate booking channels. See our refund policy and community guidelines.
