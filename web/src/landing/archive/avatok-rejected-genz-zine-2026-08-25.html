More than a streaming app

Four ways to turn your personality into a business.

Start with one. Add the others when your audience asks for more.

01 · Ticketed live

Make any moment a paid event.

Schedule a stream, publish it in the marketplace, share your link, and let fans buy access. Travel, worship, performance, teaching, behind-the-scenes—anything that works live.

Example: “Ride to Ladakh with me” · ₹50/hour
02 · 1:1 bookings

Let followers book your time.

Create a service, open calendar slots, set the duration and price, and meet inside avaTOK. Great for consultants, coaches, creators, mentors, listeners, and companions.

Example: “20-minute portfolio teardown” · ₹499
03 · AI voice agents

Create a voice that earns when you are offline.

Train an AI personality on your knowledge and style, list it in the marketplace, and charge fans to talk. Build an interview coach, AI consultant, language partner, character, or virtual companion.

Example: “Mock interview with Arjun AI” · ₹8/minute
04 · Private communication

Be reachable without sharing your number.

Get a virtual number for chat and calls. Let AI screen unknown callers, speak to them, take a message, and send you the recording—while your personal number stays private.
