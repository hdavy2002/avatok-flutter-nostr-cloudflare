How avaTOK works

avaTOK is a marketplace for your time and your attention. Sign up free, create a free listing, and get paid to do something people already need: listen to someone's stress, be a home friend, have a chat or a discussion, give advice, go on a virtual date — or simply live stream your life. People subscribe to your calendar, pick a slot and book you. That is the whole thing.

You do not need followers, a studio, a degree or an interview. A phone and a quiet corner at home is enough. Influencers and creators do well here because they arrive with an audience — but the marketplace is built so a first-time listener with zero followers can take a booking on day one. You set your own price, your own hours, and you can stop whenever you like.

  1. STEP 1

    Sign up free

    No joining fee, no equipment to buy, no fixed shift. Just an account.

  2. STEP 2

    Create a free listing

    Say what you offer — listening, a chat, a home friend, advice, a date, a live stream — and set your price.

  3. STEP 3

    Open your calendar

    Mark the hours you are free. People subscribe to your calendar and book the slot that suits them.

  4. STEP 4

    Talk, or go live

    Take the call or chat at the booked time, or live stream your day, a trek, a hike, a puja, a cooking channel — anything.

  5. STEP 5

    Get paid

    Earnings arrive in your avaTOK wallet in tokens (1 token = ₹1) and withdraw to your bank over UPI.

  • PRIVACY · SOON

    Show up on your own terms

    Blur or replace your background, drop a privacy mask over your face, go as an anonymous avatar, or put on a fun filter. Take video bookings without showing your home — or your face.

  • ON THE RECORD

    Either side can record

    Record a session if you want a copy of it. A Recording indicator stays on both screens the whole time, audio only — video is never captured — and it saves to your inbox with 5 GB free storage.

  • EVERY LANGUAGE

    Talk across languages

    Live translation runs on calls, so a booking is never limited to people who speak your language. You speak yours, they hear theirs — Hindi, Tamil, Bengali, Marathi, English and more.

Can I really make money sitting at home?

Yes — that is what avaTOK is for. Work from home, part time or full time, from any city or village in India. You are paid per session, per minute or per ticketed live event, at a price you set yourself.

What kinds of listings do people book?

Someone to listen when work or family is heavy, a home friend to talk to in the evening, a conversation in your own language, tuition, advice, a consultation, a virtual date — and live streams of treks, hikes, pujas, cooking, music, travel and everyday life.

Is it free to list?

Signing up and creating a listing are both free. avaTOK takes a share only when you actually get paid, so there is nothing to lose by putting a listing up today.

Is it safe?

Identity is verified, payments are held in escrow until a session is delivered, and your phone number is never shown to the people who book you. Community guidelines are enforced.
