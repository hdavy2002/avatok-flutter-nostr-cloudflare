The full policy

This page is the plain-English version. The binding terms — what’s refundable, what isn’t, and your rights under consumer law — live in Refunds & Cancellations; always defer to that page if anything here seems to disagree with it.

Payments are still in testing

avaTOK’s payment gateway is not fully live yet, and payment and payout flows are being tested with a small number of invited users. During this period, if anything is charged to you in error, or a paid feature doesn’t work, tell us and we’ll refund it in full — email support@avatok.ai. That’s more generous than the standing rules below, and it only applies while we’re in this testing phase; see Refunds & Cancellations §2 for the exact wording.

Creator no-show

If you book and pay for a live show or a 1:1 session and the creator never turns up, the system checks who was actually connected once the booking’s scheduled time has passed. If there’s no sign the creator ever joined, your booking is cancelled as a creator no-show and your tokens (including any tax charged) are automatically returned to your balance or refunded. If the evidence is unclear either way, it’s held for a person to review rather than resolved automatically — you don’t need to do anything, but you can also email us to flag it.
