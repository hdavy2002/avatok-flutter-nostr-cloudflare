About this havan

Sit at the feet of Shiva, the first teacher

Dakshinamurthy is Lord Shiva as the supreme teacher. He is prayed to for deep understanding — the kind of knowing that goes beyond memorising.

Tradition tells of four great sages who came to the young Shiva seated under a banyan tree, seeking the highest truth. He taught them in complete silence, and in that silence all their doubts dissolved…
