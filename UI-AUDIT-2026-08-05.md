# AvaTOK — Why the app feels amateur

**A plain-English UI audit** · 5 August 2026 · production code on `main`

---

## The one-paragraph answer

Your app doesn't feel amateur because of any single bad screen. It feels amateur because **there is no single design**. Eight different visual "rulebooks" are live in the app at the same time, and screens borrow from whichever one the developer had open that day. On top of that, three specific choices are working against you everywhere at once: **every word in the app is bold**, **almost every shape is a rounded pill**, and **two different icon sets are mixed on the same screen**. Fix those three things and the app will look dramatically more expensive before you touch a single layout.

The animations you noticed are real, but they're the *smallest* part of the problem. I found only three genuinely bouncy animations in the whole app. What actually reads as "bouncy" to your eye is the **shape and weight of everything**: fat rounded corners + very bold rounded lettering + candy colours. That combination is what people recognise as a kids' app.

# Part 1 — The five things doing the most damage

## 1. Every single word in the app is bold. There is no normal text.

This is the biggest one, and it's a two-line fix.

The app only ships four font files: **SemiBold, Bold, ExtraBold, and Black.** There is no Regular. There is no Medium. That means when the code asks for "normal body text," the phone physically cannot draw it — the lightest thing available is SemiBold.

Then the design system makes it worse by *asking* for heavy weights on top of that:

| Weight used | How many times |
|---|---|
| ExtraBold (800) | 119 |
| Bold (700) | 68 |
| SemiBold (600) | 54 |
| Black (900) | 37 |
| Medium (500) | 7 |
| **Regular (400)** | **0** |

In the wallet's own style sheet, **21 of 28 text styles are ExtraBold** — the £56-sized balance number and the 11px timestamp caption underneath it are the *same weight*.

**Why this makes it feel amateur:** professional apps create hierarchy with *contrast* — a bold headline sitting next to light grey body text. When everything is bold, nothing is emphasised, so the eye has nowhere to rest. It reads as shouting. It's the visual equivalent of writing an email IN CAPS.

**Example you can see right now:** open a chat. The contact's name is ExtraBold. The message preview underneath is SemiBold. The timestamp is SemiBold. The unread badge is Black. Four things, all fighting for attention, none of them winning.

*Files: `app/pubspec.yaml` (fonts block), `app/lib/core/ui/avatok_dark.dart` (ADText), `app/lib/features/wallet/wallet_theme.dart` (AWText)*

## 2. Eight different design systems are live at once

This is the structural cause of "it doesn't feel like one app."

| Rulebook | Lives in | What it looks like |
|---|---|---|
| **Zine** | `core/ui/zine.dart` | Cream paper, thick black 2.5px borders, hard black offset shadows, lime green buttons |
| **AD** (AvaTOK Dark v2) | `core/ui/avatok_dark.dart` | Near-black, thin hairline borders, soft blurred shadows, multicoloured icons |
| **AW** | `features/wallet/wallet_theme.dart` | A *third* near-black palette with its own colour names |
| **BubbleTheme** | `core/ui/bubble_theme.dart` | Chat bubbles |
| **AvaDialTheme** | `features/avadial/avadial_theme.dart` | Dialer |
| **PhoneTheme** | `features/avaphone/phone_theme.dart` | Phone |
| **IntentTheme** | `features/marketplace/intent_theme.dart` | Marketplace |
| **LiveTheme** | `features/identity/liveness_v2/live_theme.dart` | ID verification |

Zine and AD are **direct opposites** — one is a light cream paper look, the other is near-black. A migration from Zine to AD was started and never finished:

- **94 files** still use the old light Zine look
- **154 files** use the new dark AD look
- **9 files use both in the same file**

**Example you can see right now:** on the chat list, tap the **+** button to start a new chat. The four rows in that menu — Chat with Ava, New chat, New group, Invite friends — have **cream-paper coloured icon badges from the light design system**, sitting on a near-black sheet, with dark-system text next to them. Two different apps, one bottom sheet. (`features/avatok/chat_list.dart:1480–1497`)

**Example two:** the Wallet screen. The black header bar at the top is drawn with the AD system. Everything you scroll below it is drawn with the AW system — which uses *pure black* borders and *thick 2.5px* outlines with hard offset drop-shadows, i.e. the sticker look from the old Zine system. The header and the body are from different eras of the app. (`features/wallet/wallet_screen.dart:30–77` vs `968` onward)

## 3. Two icon sets are mixed on nearly every screen

The app uses **Material Icons** (Google's chunky, filled, geometric set — 1,985 uses) *and* **Phosphor Icons** (a thin, hand-drawn-feeling outline set — 1,651 uses).

**194 files use both.**

These two sets have completely different line thickness, corner style, and optical weight. Putting them side by side is instantly noticeable even to people who can't say why.

**Example you can see right now — the chat list screen:**

- The **+** floating button icon → Material (`Icons.add_rounded`)
- The **hamburger menu** icon → Material (`Icons.menu_rounded`)
- The **Chats / Groups / Calls** tab icons → Material
- Every single **menu row, badge, and header icon around them** → Phosphor

So the three most-looked-at controls on your busiest screen are drawn in a different hand from everything surrounding them. (`features/avatok/chat_list.dart:1664, 1701, 2047–2049`)

**Example two — the home screen:** the cards you scroll use Phosphor glyphs; the tab bar pinned directly below them uses Material glyphs. They are touching each other on screen.

## 4. Everything is a pill, and the corners never agree

Rounded corners in the app:

| Corner radius | Times used |
|---|---|
| **100 (full pill)** | **233** |
| 14 | 53 |
| 12 | 27 |
| 3 | 15 |
| 10 | 15 |
| 8 | 14 |
| 6 | 13 |
| 16 | 12 |
| …and 10 more values | (18 distinct radii in total) |

Two separate problems here.

**a) Too many pills.** 233 fully-rounded pill shapes is the single strongest "playful app" signal in the codebase. Pills are great for *one* thing — a status chip, a tag. When buttons, badges, chips, counters, handles, and containers are *all* pills, the interface loses structure. Serious apps (Revolut, Linear, WhatsApp, Apple) use pills sparingly and rely on gently-rounded rectangles (8–12px) for everything else.

**b) The radii don't form a scale.** 18 different values, including oddities like 3, 6, 7, 9, 15, and 19.5. There's no rule a designer could state out loud.

**Example you can see right now — the Wallet screen alone uses nine different corner radii:** 11, 14, 15, 16, 18, 20, 24, 26, and 100. On one screen. A card at 20px sits next to a card at 24px sits next to a button at 15px. You can't consciously see the 4px difference, but your brain registers it as "sloppy."

## 5. Colours are hardcoded, not chosen

**260 unique hardcoded colours** are typed directly into screens (415 total occurrences), on top of the eight theme files that already define colours properly.

That means a colour on one screen is *near* but not *equal* to the same-meaning colour on another screen — two slightly different greens for "online," two slightly different reds for "error." Again, invisible individually, obvious in aggregate.

Colours are also being used decoratively rather than meaningfully. The dark theme assigns a **different colour to almost every icon**: search is blue, bell is orange, shield is green, phone is teal, video is purple, camera is pink, emoji is yellow, mic is deep purple. That's eight colours in one toolbar. In professional apps, colour *means* something (unread, error, money, live). Here it's decoration, so it can no longer signal anything.

# Part 2 — The animation situation (smaller than you think)

You mentioned bouncy animations. Here's what's actually there:

**Genuinely bouncy (overshoot) animations: only 3 in the whole app.**

- The call picture-in-picture thumbnail springs in (`easeOutBack`)
- Tapping an avatar to enlarge it springs open (`easeOutBack`)
- The ID-verification success tick does a full elastic pop (`elasticOut`) — this one is the most cartoonish

**Always-on looping animations: 16.** These are the ones that make an interface feel restless. The notable ones:

- **A rotating "comet" ring spins around every contact avatar that has a live status, forever, on your busiest scrolling screen** (`features/status/status_ring.dart:33–39`). One spinning animation per visible row. The code comment even acknowledges the risk: a fast spin would "read as a loading spinner." A slow spin still reads as *something is happening* on a screen where nothing is happening.
- The ID-verification flow has **six separate looping animations** pulsing at once
- Chat and call screens each run their own permanent loops

**Glows.** Two decorative glows on the chat list: an orange halo under the **+** button, and a green halo ring around your own status avatar. Coloured glows behind buttons are a strong 2014-era mobile-game signal.

**Speeds are uncoordinated: 25+ different animation durations** across the app (80ms, 120ms, 150ms, 160ms, 180ms, 200ms, 220ms, 250ms, 300ms, 320ms, 350ms, 400ms, 450ms, 500ms, 600ms, 700ms, 800ms, 900ms, 1200ms, 1400ms, 1500ms, 1800ms, 2200ms, 2500ms…). Professional apps use three: fast (~120ms), normal (~200ms), slow (~320ms). When speeds are random, transitions feel unrelated to each other.

**Bottom line on animation:** cutting the three overshoot curves and the two glows takes an hour and removes the most childish moments. But it will not, on its own, fix the "amateur" feeling — items 1–5 above will.

# Part 3 — Smaller things that add up

**Emoji used as interface elements.** Emoji appear inside system copy in dozens of places — not as user reactions, but as icons the app draws for itself:

- `'🎉 Your vision agent is live!'`
- `'📹 Video call started — tap 📞 to join'`
- `'📷 Photo'`, `'🎬 Video'`, `'🎙️ Voice message'`, `'📎 file'` in chat previews
- `'Ava ✨'` (the sparkle is part of the product name in 4 places)
- `'🌍 All'` in the marketplace filter
- `'Backed up to your AvaTOK Drive (Backups) ✓'`
- `'📞 💼 ✈️ 🤫'` as the four options on the incoming business call screen

Emoji render differently on every device, can't be recoloured, don't align with your icon set, and don't scale with text properly. They are the fastest way to make a paid product look like a weekend project. The business-call screen is the worst case — four emoji as the primary action buttons on a screen a customer sees when a client rings them.

**ALL CAPS shouting.** 161 uppercase labels across 94 files, including a `'AVATOK · YOU OWN IT ALL'` slogan at the bottom of Settings. All-caps is a poster technique; in a phone UI it reads as aggressive.

**Nunito is the wrong typeface for this product.** Nunito is a *rounded terminal* font — the ends of every letter stroke are literally circular. It was designed to feel soft and friendly. Combined with everything being ExtraBold and everything being pill-shaped, it is doing more than any other single choice to create the "bouncy, amateur" impression. This is a communications and money app; it should look like a tool.

**Spacing has no grid.** 34 different gap sizes are used between elements, including 1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, 26, 34. Professional layouts use a 4px or 8px grid — so 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32 and nothing else. Right now vertical rhythm is arbitrary, which is why screens feel slightly "off" without an obvious reason.

**Type sizes have no scale.** 36 distinct font sizes, including 8.5, 9.5, 10.5, 11.5, 12.5, 13.5, 14.5, 15.5, 16.5, 19.5. Half-pixel sizes are a sign of tuning by eye rather than by system.

**One file is 13,476 lines long.** `features/avatok/chat_thread.dart` is your chat screen. At that size nobody can hold it in their head, which is *why* the design drifts — a developer adding a feature copies the nearest block of styling rather than reaching for the design system. This is the mechanism by which all of the above got worse over time.

# Part 4 — The fix plan

Ordered by **visual payoff per hour of work.** Items 1–4 will change how the app feels more than everything else combined, and none of them require redesigning a single screen.

## Tier 1 — Do these first (biggest change, least risk)

### Fix 1. Bundle a Regular font weight and demote body text
**Impact: massive. Effort: ~2 hours. Risk: very low.**

- Add `Nunito-Regular.ttf` (400) and `Nunito-Medium.ttf` (500) to `app/assets/fonts/`, register both in `app/pubspec.yaml`
- In `core/ui/avatok_dark.dart` (`ADText`) and `features/wallet/wallet_theme.dart` (`AWText`): change every body, subtitle, caption, preview, timestamp and metadata style from w800/w700 → **w400**
- Keep w700 for screen titles and w600 for row names only. Nothing in the app should be w800 or w900 except a wallet balance.

*This single change does more than anything else on this list.*

### Fix 2. Replace Nunito with a neutral typeface
**Impact: massive. Effort: ~3 hours. Risk: low (visual only).**

Swap Nunito for **Inter** — the default choice for serious modern product UI (Linear, Vercel, GitHub, Notion-adjacent). It is free, has every weight, and is a drop-in replacement: change the four filenames and `ZineText.display/body/mono` + `ADText`'s family constant. Alternatives if you want warmth without the roundness: **Manrope** or **Plus Jakarta Sans**.

*Do Fix 1 and Fix 2 together in the same emulator session and screenshot before/after — the difference will be obvious immediately.*

### Fix 3. Kill the pills
**Impact: high. Effort: ~4 hours. Risk: low.**

Define exactly four radii and use nothing else:

| Token | Value | For |
|---|---|---|
| `rSm` | 8 | chips, badges, small controls |
| `rMd` | 12 | buttons, inputs, list rows |
| `rLg` | 16 | cards, dialogs |
| `rPill` | 999 | **only** avatars, status dots, and the drag handle on a sheet |

Then find-and-replace the 233 `BorderRadius.circular(100)` uses — keep it only where the thing is genuinely circular or a genuine tag. Delete the 18-value radius spread by mapping every existing value to the nearest of the four.

### Fix 4. Pick one icon set
**Impact: high. Effort: ~6 hours (mechanical). Risk: low.**

Standardise on **Phosphor**, since it's already the design-system default and covers more of the app's needs (it has 1,651 uses to Material's 1,985, but Material's are concentrated in a few high-traffic spots). Replace the Material icons in these places first, because they're the most-seen:

- `features/avatok/chat_list.dart:1664` (the **+** button)
- `features/avatok/chat_list.dart:1701` (hamburger)
- `features/avatok/chat_list.dart:2047–2049` (Chats/Groups/Calls tabs)
- `shell/v2/app_switcher_bar.dart:116–125, 273, 289` (the whole bottom nav)

Then sweep the remaining 190 files. Add a CI lint that fails the build on `import 'package:flutter/material.dart' show Icons` in feature code.

## Tier 2 — Consolidation (finish what was started)

### Fix 5. Finish the Zine → AD migration and delete Zine
**Impact: high. Effort: ~2 weeks. Risk: medium — touches 94 files.**

The two systems are opposites; every screen still on Zine looks like a different app. Sequence:

1. **First, fix the 9 files that mix both in one file** — these are the most visibly broken. Start with `features/avatok/chat_list.dart:1480–1497` (the new-chat sheet), then `features/settings/settings_screen.dart`, `shell/ava_sidebar.dart`, `features/onboarding/onboarding_flow.dart`.
2. Migrate the remaining 94 Zine files feature by feature, highest-traffic first.
3. Delete `core/ui/zine.dart` and `AvaColors` from `core/theme.dart` so nobody can reach for them again.

### Fix 6. Fold AW, BubbleTheme, AvaDialTheme, PhoneTheme, IntentTheme and LiveTheme into AD
**Impact: high. Effort: ~1 week. Risk: medium.**

`AW` (wallet) is the priority — it's a whole parallel dark palette with its own colour names, and the wallet is a screen people judge you on. Its hard-offset sticker shadows (`Offset(6,7)`, `Offset(4,5)`, 2.5px pure-black borders) are the neo-brutalist look from the old Zine era and should go entirely. The others can be reduced to thin "which AD accent does this feature use" mappings rather than full palettes.

### Fix 7. Delete the 260 hardcoded colours
**Impact: medium. Effort: ~3 days. Risk: low.**

Sweep `Color(0x...)` out of feature files into AD tokens. Add a CI lint rejecting raw hex outside `core/ui/`. While doing this, **cut the multicolour icon palette** — search-blue, bell-orange, shield-green, phone-teal, video-purple, camera-pink, emoji-yellow, mic-purple should collapse to one neutral icon colour plus one accent, with colour reserved for meaning (unread, error, money, live).

## Tier 3 — Polish

### Fix 8. One motion scale, and cut the loops
**Impact: medium. Effort: ~1 day. Risk: low.**

- Define three durations only: `fast 120ms`, `normal 200ms`, `slow 320ms`. Replace all 25+ values.
- Ban `Curves.elasticOut`, `bounceOut`, `easeOutBack` in feature code. The three uses are at `core/calls/call_pip_thumbnail.dart:90`, `core/avatar_viewer.dart:35`, `features/identity/liveness_v2/stage_chrome.dart:554`. Replace with `Curves.easeOutCubic`.
- Stop `features/status/status_ring.dart` spinning forever in a scrolling list — make it a static ring, or animate only on first appearance.
- Remove the two coloured glows: `features/avatok/chat_list.dart:1661` (orange FAB halo) and `:2005` (green avatar ring).
- Reduce the ID-verification flow's six simultaneous loops to at most one.

### Fix 9. Remove emoji from app-authored copy
**Impact: medium. Effort: ~1 day. Risk: none.**

Replace with Phosphor icons. Priority order: `features/avatok/incoming_business_call_screen.dart:122–126` (emoji as primary buttons on a business call), then chat previews in `chat_thread.dart:4288–4291`, then `'Ava ✨'` in `features/ava/ava_lane.dart`, then the rest.

### Fix 10. Adopt a spacing and type scale
**Impact: medium. Effort: ~3 days. Risk: low.**

- Spacing: **4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48 only.** Map the 34 current values onto these.
- Type: **12, 14, 16, 18, 22, 28, 34 only.** Delete every half-pixel size.
- Drop the 161 `toUpperCase()` calls to sentence case, including the `'AVATOK · YOU OWN IT ALL'` footer in Settings.

### Fix 11. Break up `chat_thread.dart`
**Impact: indirect but important. Effort: ~1 week. Risk: medium.**

13,476 lines is the *reason* the design drifts. Split into composer, bubble, media, header, and call-banner files. Until this happens, every fix above will slowly erode in this file.

---

## Suggested first session

If you want the single highest-return afternoon:

1. Add Regular + Medium font weights (Fix 1)
2. Swap Nunito → Inter (Fix 2)
3. Demote all body text to w400 (Fix 1)
4. Replace the four Material icons on the chat list and bottom nav (Fix 4, partial)
5. Collapse the 233 pills to the four-radius scale (Fix 3)

That's one emulator session with hot reload — no CI build needed — and it will change how the whole app feels.
